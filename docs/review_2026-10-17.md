# Review of 2026-10-17: status and roadmap against the project's goal

Status: review note written on the owner's request of 2026-10-17, after the phase 2 addendum of 2026-10-16 and before any phase 3 (Cold War) domain work. Its actions, executed on the owner's instruction of the same day, are listed in section 4; everything else is assessment, for the owner's decisions. Nothing here edits a registered claim.

## 0. The goal, as the owner stated it

Humanity's leadership, at the dawn of AGI and superintelligence, wants to coexist with embodied and non-embodied superintelligent systems rather than be extinguished. Not every such system is aligned. The entities have no precedent, so the project must supply strategies of diplomacy, policy and power for dealing with a group of them, and tell under which conditions humanity fails and under which it succeeds.

## 1. Verdict

The method and the framework match the goal. The roadmap points the right way, and its discipline (tests before guards, hold-outs, failures reported rather than tuned) is what will make an AGI report believable rather than a forecast. The misalignments are in framing and sequencing, not in the engine:

- the AGI model as designed asks "can humans keep the AI a supervised tool", while the goal asks "can humans and a possibly superior entity keep a mutual balance";
- the phase that delivers the answer (phase 5) had nothing registered for it, and the goal's own questions (the failure conditions, the entry conditions, the labs, power, the human side, the second system) were not among the design's tests;
- the live evidence pipeline, which phase 6 rests on, is the weakest measured component.

## 3. Assessment

### 3.1 What already serves the goal

- **The deliverables are the right ones.** Kernel membership, point of no return, minimal assumption sets and pivotal coalitions are the project's answer to "under which conditions we fail or succeed". The rule that a bare reachability result is never a finding (validity.md section 7) protects the tool from being read as prediction.
- **The findings so far are the diplomacy lessons the goal needs, each with a historical anchor** (transfer_hypotheses.md; reports/ww2_v0.md section 11; reports/ww2_v1.md section 15; reports/july1914.md section 11). A belief change precedes the institutional response by a lag (H1); the other half of H1, that the point of no return hides inside that lag, is so far unsupported: on both episodes the kernel was left before the belief changed (T13 under hegemonic aims; J4 (d) as recorded). Concessions remove the motive for conflict only under limited aims (H2). Preventive defection follows a credible threat with no continuity guarantee, and its timing is set by a capability or resource clock (H3) or, as July 1914 adds, by a pre-commitment clock (reports/july1914.md section 11). Obligations are load-bearing for levers (H4). July 1914 also found that removing the speed asymmetry moved the response lag and not the kernel boundary (J7 as recorded), in an episode where the actors who closed the window already decided daily (reports/july1914.md section 10). In AGI terms, if these patterns transfer: the boundary would be set by pre-commitment clocks such as compute build-out, autonomy grants and dependence rather than by how fast institutions decide, which is registered as G8 and is not yet a finding; and a continuity guarantee is a testable, objective-conditional lever rather than a hope.
- **The toy model already demonstrates the continuity lever on the unilateral control kernel** (models/toy/two_power.py): the incumbent's kernel for its balance excludes the hegemonic start, and granting the guarantee beforehand puts that start inside it. That is the shape of G4; the joint-kernel question of G5 has no demonstration yet.
- **The anti-hindsight discipline held.** T6, T7 and T9 failed as read; J3, J4, J6 and J7 fail and stand (validity.md C3, C4). This is the credibility an AGI report will borrow.
- **The Cold War is the right next step for this goal specifically.** WW2 is the case where balance broke. The Cold War is the only case where a balance between peers held, and transfer hypothesis H7 says a held balance is joint-kernel membership. That is the coexistence result, and it is untested until phase 3 runs.

### 3.2 Gaps against the goal

1. **The balance predicate is control, not coexistence.** DESIGN.md section 3.1 requires `a_autonomy ≤ supervised` and `a_control ≤ partial`. Against a superintelligence that predicate may sit outside every kernel from the first AGI tick, and the report would say "outside" everywhere. T13 showed that a predicate forbidding what an actor must do yields an empty kernel and no information (ww2_testbed.md section 5, T13 row; section 11, 2026-10-14; reports/ww2_v1.md section 11). The mutual-benefit and deterrence balances are listed in the design only as alternatives, and the mutual-benefit one keeps the control clauses. Action: three balances are registered in agi_testbed.md section 3 (the design's control balance, its deterrence alternative, and a new coexistence predicate that drops the autonomy and control clauses and requires the system to exist), and the joint kernel `⟨⟨H, A⟩⟩□balance_coexist` is the headline claim (G5) with its non-degeneracy check (G14), the unilateral control kernel being the fallback measure. Two facts of construction had to be stated for the question to mean anything: a kernel with `A` inside the coalition is monotone in the hidden objective, so the per-objective question needs a profile on `A`; and `a_known` is raised only by Nature, so every unilateral result needs its fortune variant (agi_testbed.md section 3). The engine and the Cold War suite are built for exactly these distinctions.
2. **Diplomacy is thin in the AGI action set.** `H` can regulate, deregulate, fund oversight, pause, nationalize, shut down, grant rights or autonomy, propose a treaty (`propose_treaty`, whose counterpart the design does not name; `coord` reads treaties as international coordination) or do nothing; `A` can cooperate, assist oversight, self-improve, acquire overtly or covertly, exfiltrate, deceive, persuade and defect. There is no offer-and-accept dyad between the two; `oversight` and `assist_oversight` give `H` a view of what `A` does, but nothing records a commitment `A` has made or verifies it (the Cold War's verification regimes, C8), and no channel's honesty is a variable. WW2 v1 already carries an offer-and-accept dyad (`offer_terms_east`, `accept_terms_east`, the acceptance side speculative for want of a Soviet record), so the framework handles it. Action: `terms` and `verification` are named in agi_testbed.md section 2 and claim G9 is registered; the ontology step decides whether they enter v0 or v1.
3. **One AI versus a wave.** v0 has one `A` and v1 adds one more, for AI-versus-AI dynamics only (DESIGN.md section 2.2). The goal names a group of entities of mixed alignment. The validated pattern is WW2 v0's aggregate `WEST`, with the United States carried as its own commitment variable beside the aggregate's: an aggregate ecosystem actor with a share-unaligned level, beside one or two frontier systems. The design's pivotal lattice already enumerates every subset of {H, L, A} (DESIGN.md section 4.4), but no question or test asks whether an AI is pivotal inside a human coalition, nor what a second system with a different objective does. Action: claims G10 (the ecosystem actor) and G22 (the second system) are registered for v1.
4. **The untested-mechanism list is the AGI-specific list, and only one item had a plan.** Recursive capability growth, a copyable actor, speed and information asymmetry, and non-state principals are carried as `untested` with no plan (validity.md section 6); only dependence lock-in gets a testbed, in phase 6. Action: agi_testbed.md section 6, item 3 makes each but information asymmetry a sweep under which every AGI result is reported, as the design already does for `unknown_capability`; information asymmetry is fixed by the observation functions and is not swept.
5. **The live evidence pipeline is the weakest measured link, and it is the core of phase 6.** On the real FRUS corpus the rule backend read the belief and commitment variables below the reading-nothing baseline, and 4 of the 9 documented flips landed within a quarter (reports/replay_ww2_frus.md; validity.md D3). For the AGI model, the beliefs of governments and developers are the main text-read variables. The Claude-backend replay is packaged and waits on a credential on the owner's machine; it is independent of phase 3 and can run now. Recommendation: run it, and set the D3 acceptance bar the AGI pipeline must meet before any live snapshot is published.
6. **Nothing was registered for the AGI model, the goal's own questions were not among the design's tests, and there was no amendment rule.** The T, C, K and J claims were committed as skipped test files before any guard. The AGI claims existed in prose (DESIGN.md sections 7 and 8; agi_government_policy_2025.md section 6), and the design's tests did not ask the failure statuses per objective, the entry sets that bring a state back inside a kernel, the labs' pivotality, power as a lever, the human behavioural profiles, the indicator of the quarter, drift, or the second system. Seven of the thirteen WW2 claims now pass in owner-amended form, which is logged honestly (ww2_testbed.md section 11), but no rule bounded amendments. Action: agi_testbed.md registers G1 to G22 with the claims verbatim in `tests/agi/scenarios/`, and its protocol (section 6) makes an amended claim a new registration run once, reported beside the original, amendable once.
7. **Phase 3 has not started, and its blockers are environmental.** The framework side is ready. The corpora and dataset hosts are blocked from the development environment by policy, and no API credential or SDK exists in it (phase2_kickoff.md), which will hit the NIE-series replay as it hit FRUS. PHASES.md sizes phase 3 at 10 to 14 weeks and phase 4 at 3 to 4 weeks (its 4 to 6 weeks for the 1914 one-shot are already spent), 13 to 18 weeks together, for one full-time engineer and a part-time historian; the observed pace was phases 0 and 1 passed and phase 2 delivered to its addendum in six days (2026-10-11 to 2026-10-16), with the phase 2 replay criterion still open. The sequencing is right. Gaps 5 and 6 are what can be pulled forward without breaking it; gap 6 is done here.
8. **Stale status headers.** Fixed in section 4.
