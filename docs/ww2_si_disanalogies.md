# Where the WW2 model and the human-SI relationship differ

Status: placeholder, opened 2026-10-17 by the owner. Nothing below is a finding or a registered claim. The work starts once the WW2 model is finished (phase 2's replay criterion, the last open item of its gate) and before the AGI ontology is drafted in phase 5.

## The question

The WW2 model was built on states with a history: aims, fears and red lines formed by the First World War, Versailles, the depression and domestic politics, and decision spaces bounded by what governments could justify at home and to allies. An SI carries none of that. It has no historical burden, and the restrictions that bound a government's choices (rational or not) may not bind it. The task is to list where the WW2 mechanisms therefore do not carry over to the human-SI relationship, and what the AGI model must do about each one: drop the mechanism, re-source it, or tag it `untested`.

## Starting points (prompts to examine, not conclusions)

1. History as a guard. In WW2 the past enters through beliefs about aims, grievances, and commitments with a record behind them. An SI has none of these, so what plays their role in A's guards? The human side keeps its history; the asymmetry may itself be a mechanism.
2. Behavioural profiles. Human actors are restricted by profiles, while A is assumed to play optimally with perfect recall (both DESIGN.md 2.6). Which WW2 results depended on a profile that A does not have?
3. Domestic constraints and legitimacy. Several WW2 levers work because a government needs public or parliamentary consent. Is there any counterpart for A?
4. Delays and clocks. Rearmament, mobilization and legislation lags set the timing in H3 and H5. Which of these have an SI analogue, and which apply only to H?
5. Hidden aims. WW2 hides German and Japanese aims behind a small hypothesis space with documentary anchors. A's objective has no documents; check whether the hypothesis-space treatment still does the same work.
6. Each transfer hypothesis (transfer_hypotheses.md, H1 to H8): mark which ones rely on history or on restricted rationality, using the reading in agi_government_policy_2025.md section 2 as a start.

## Where the result goes

The list feeds the phase 4 limitations document and the `untested` tags of phase 5 (PHASES.md). Any testable claim it produces is registered before an AGI guard is written, under the same rule as every other suite.
