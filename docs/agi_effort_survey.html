How hard is each of these moves to make?

We are modelling how governments, AI labs and an advanced AI system might act toward each other over the next fifteen years, and for each move we need to know how much effort it costs the one who makes it: political capital, money, organisational strain or lost revenue, compared with what they would do anyway. This is not how harmful or how likely the move is. Below is where we have put each move. Which ones would you put in a different column?

No effortDoing nothing, or what their incentives already push them to do.
Low effortA routine decision within ordinary powers and budget.
Mid effortA contested decision: needs a coalition, a large budget or a visible sacrifice.
Great effortAn exceptional decision: emergency powers, an international agreement, or giving up the core business.
