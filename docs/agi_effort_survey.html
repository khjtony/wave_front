How hard is each of these moves to make?

We are building a model of how governments, AI labs and an advanced AI system might act toward each other, quarter by quarter, over the next fifteen years. Each can choose among a short list of moves.

For every move the model needs one judgment: how much effort does it take the one who makes it? That means political capital, money, organisational strain or lost revenue, measured against what they would do if simply left to their incentives. It is not how harmful the move is, and not how likely.

So far these scores are one person's judgment, and our results turn out to depend on them. We would like yours. The table below shows where we put each move. Move any you think is in the wrong column, then send us your version at the bottom.

0The defaultDoing nothing, or what their incentives already push them to do.
1RoutineA decision within ordinary powers and budget.
2ContestedNeeds a coalition, a large budget or a visible sacrifice.
3ExceptionalEmergency powers, an international agreement, or giving up the core business.
