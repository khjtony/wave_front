AGI v0.5: a faster clock and a prediction mode

The owner's decisions of 25 to 27 November, what follows from them, and the five claims registered on 27 November before any rule of v0.5 was written. The prediction mode and the effort rule are designed here and are not yet registered.

1 · What is new

  • Two-month steps, ending in 2032. One step is two months instead of a quarter, and the study runs from November 2026 to the end of 2032 instead of 2040.
  • A prediction mode. From Srt, the likelihood of each ending and the most likely routes, given the model, an effort rule and the owner's priors over the AI goals.
  • Effort that depends on the state. What an action costs its player changes with alarm, reliance, rivalry and the industry's own stance.
  • Otherwise a copy of v0.4 with both AI systems on.

5 · The prediction mode

  • Start: Srt; where a reading is uncertain, a weighted mix of the candidate levels.
  • Each step: each player picks among its available actions with a chance that falls with the action's effort in the current state; chance events have base rates.
  • Likelihood of an ending: the share of paths that reach it by a date (2028, 2030, 2032), per AI goal and combined under each profile, as a range over the modelling choices.
  • Most likely routes: the highest-likelihood paths, grouped by their key events, each with its share and a real-world reading.
  • Leverage of an action on an ending: the change in that ending's likelihood when the action's effort moves by one step. Presence (how much more often the action is on paths to the ending) is shown beside it.
  • Beside every likelihood: the guarantee-zone reading where one exists. Likely and preventable are different questions.

Needs a rule change. The project rule is that reports never quote a bare path result as a finding. A likelihood given the model is a new kind of deliverable; it needs an entry in the conventions and the framework specification, with the owner's approval, before the first prediction report.

  • The regulation ladder is scored step by step: advocating, a voluntary accord, a binding rule and a treaty do not cost the same.
  • News moves the state through scored, two-sided evidence; a fixed rule turns the state into effort. Scores are not changed by hand when news arrives.
  • Calibration: a replay of past decisions as a path asks whether each real decision was available in the model and how likely the rule made it. Few parameters are fitted; results of registered claims are never used.
  • Effort scores are restated per two months.

7 · Settled and still open

  • Settled on 27 November: the profile's name, one frontier system, the reading of the open-weight prior (decisions 9 to 11).
  • Open: the numbers of the effort rule; whether the past is replayed from a backdated start state; the entry in the conventions for a likelihood as a deliverable. The claims of the prediction mode are registered with the effort rule, before its first run.
