AGI v0.3 (draft): the open-weight gap

v0.2 treats an open-weight release as one event. On the owner's decisions of 18 November, v0.3 replaces it with a standing gap: an open-weight system follows the labs' system a quarter or two behind, a shutdown cannot reach it, and it presses the closed labs to race.

Draft. No guard, model file, claim or job exists for v0.3 and nothing has been run. v0, v0.1 and v0.2 results stand as computed. Basis: docs/agi_open_weights_review.html and the survey behind it.

3 · The second system (model v1, moved up)

On the owner's reading, the copies outside the labs are the third-party providers serving open-weight models. That gives the second system a concrete shape, and one distinction the survey supports:

  • Hosted copies, run by providers. Governments can reach these: a service can be licensed, restricted or closed. Most use of the top open models goes through providers, because the models are too large for ordinary hardware (0.9 to 1.6 terabytes).
  • Private copies, on privately owned computing power. Governments cannot reach these (decision 2). What runs on ordinary hardware trails the frontier by about a year, against one to two quarters for hosted open models.

So in v1 a government move against providers would not remove the open system; it would push the unreachable part of it back from a gap of 1 to 2 quarters to about 4. That is a real lever, and v0.3 does not have it. v0.3 keeps one AI and treats the open system as a level that follows; v1 gives it its own moves, its own objective and its providers.

4 · What I expect it to change

  • A window after each step. For one or two quarters after the labs reach AGI, a shutdown can still contain and the system cannot be retrained by the public. v0.2 had no such window: chance could open the weights in the first quarter.
  • The treaty becomes a race against the gap. A treaty before the open system reaches AGI keeps it below AGI. In the model a treaty takes two quarters and has no delay, which is as long as the gap. The treaty delay from the delay research (2 quarters) would decide this, so it should be in the same version.
  • Containment has a deadline. The "contained" ending needs a shutdown before the open system reaches AGI.
  • Pressure from the first quarter. E3 (b) found governments and labs keep the control guarantee under pressure, probably by taking over the labs at once. With pressure on from the start, that is the only way from today.

These are expectations to be turned into registered predictions, not results.

5 · Cost

Two new counters and one new four-level variable on top of v0.2, whose largest kernel already went over 26 GB. v0.3's kernels need the 128 GB machine, and a timing probe first. The bounded claims (fastest roads, existence checks) stay cheap.

6 · What the owner decides

  1. The gap: 1 quarter as the baseline with 2 beside it (proposed), or the reverse. One quarter is the general figure, two the figure for cyber capability. A shorter gap is worse for the human side.
  2. What a shutdown does once an open AGI-level system exists. Proposed: it removes the labs' system only (capability falls to the open level, control is not reset, no containment). The harsher alternative: it fails outright and turns the AI hostile, as a failed shutdown does in v0.
  3. Race pressure's trigger. Proposed: on while the open system is level with the labs', off while the labs are a level ahead. The alternative is v0.2's: on for good once any open system exists.
  4. The treaty delay in the same version (proposed, as a switch), since the gap and the treaty race each other. On 17 November the owner put it in its own version.
  5. Outside testing: keep only faster evaluation and detection (proposed), or keep it as in v0.2.
  6. Retraining toward aligned: drop it (proposed; nothing supports it), or keep it flagged as assumed.
  7. Order of work: v0.3 first, then the second system (proposed), or the second system directly.
