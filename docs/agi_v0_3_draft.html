AGI v0.3 (draft): the open-weight system as a second AI

On the owner's decisions of 18 November the model goes straight to two AI systems: the labs' closed system, as before, and an open-weight system that follows it two quarters behind, is served by third-party providers, and cannot be shut down.

Draft. No guard, model file, claim or job exists for v0.3 and nothing has been run. v0, v0.1 and v0.2 results stand as computed. Section 2 records the owner's decisions; section 4 lists the design choices still to confirm.

1 · The two systems

A · the labs' system (as in v0 to v0.2)

Closed weights

  • The most capable system; reaches each level first.
  • One of four hidden objectives, fixed for the whole play. The public cannot retrain it.
  • Works under oversight; its covert acts can be detected only where oversight exists.
  • A pause or a shutdown reaches it.
O · the open-weight system (new)

Open weights, served by third-party providers

  • Reaches each capability level 2 quarters after the labs, unless a treaty is in force.
  • Released with safeguards, which chance can strip within the quarter. Stripped, it takes control whenever it can.
  • Outside oversight, but open to inspection: its acts can always be detected.
  • No pause or shutdown reaches it. Governments can restrict the providers that host it; they cannot reach private copies.

4 · Design choices to confirm

The seven decisions settle the outline. These four are choices I made to fill it in, and each changes what the results can say:

  1. O's objective is two-valued: safeguarded or stripped. A stripped O acts like the "indifferent" objective. The alternative is to give O the same four objectives as A, which quadruples the model for a distinction the evidence does not support (stripping removes refusals; it does not install a goal).
  2. O's control is separate from A's, with its own ending. This is what lets "a shutdown removes the labs' system only" mean something. The cost is four times the states.
  3. The hosting lever. Restricting providers cuts O's reach and doubles its lag to 4 quarters. It is the one government move that touches O after release. It is grounded in the survey but its effect is my estimate. It can be left out of the first build and added as a switch.
  4. Providers do not drive adoption themselves. In this draft only the labs' deployments raise society's dependence on AI. In the world, providers serving open models do too. Adding that means a new source of dependence that no pause reaches; I have left it out to keep the first version readable.

6 · Cost

v0.3 adds about a thousand times the states of v0.1 (O's level, its count-down, its safeguards, its control, the hosting flag, the pressure counter, the pending treaty). v0.2 added twelve times and its largest kernel already went over 26 GB. So v0.3's kernels may not fit even 128 GB as drafted. The plan: build it with each new piece as a switch, run the bounded claims first, and probe kernel cost with pieces off and on before registering any kernel claim that cannot be computed. If the full model is too large, the hosting lever and O's separate control are the first candidates to simplify.
