Inputs: the registered claims ([coldwar_testbed.md](coldwar_testbed.md) section 5, committed 2026-10-11 before any WW2 or Cold War guard), the transfer hypotheses ([transfer_hypotheses.md](transfer_hypotheses.md), committed 2026-10-12 before any Cold War guard), the protocol of [ww2_testbed.md](ww2_testbed.md) section 7 and coldwar_testbed.md section 7, the "contested" reading of ww2_testbed.md section 4.6, and the framework as specified ([framework_spec.md](framework_spec.md), sections 3, 5, 7, 14 and 15). Content: [ontology_cw.md](ontology_cw.md), [actions_cw.md](actions_cw.md), `data/cw/`. Model: `models/cw/v0.py` and `models/cw/crises/`. Snapshots: `models/cw/snapshots.py`, `snapshots/cw/`.

This document was written, with the readings of section R, before any registered claim was run, with one exception disclosed in D9. Nothing here edits a registered claim. Where a claim cannot be read without a choice, the choice is made here and the report computes the registered wording; a reading that makes a claim degenerate is reported as degenerate, with the variant computed beside it, as for T13 (ww2_testbed.md section 11, 2026-10-14).

## D1. Time

- One tick is one calendar year. **The state at tick t is 1 January of year 1947 + t.** The joint move at tick t is what each actor decided during that year; its effects are visible at t + 1. The DSL horizon is 45, the state of 1 January 1992 (the state after 1991). "From year Y" in a claim is the state at tick Y − 1947.
- A reaction to another actor's decision of the same year appears the next year ([SQR]). Nature plays one event per tick; two Nature events dated in one year cannot both sit in it, and the second is placed in an adjacent year with a note ([NSL]). An actor takes one decision per year; where the record has two decisions of one actor in one year the second is placed in the next or previous year with a note (data/cw/realized_moves.csv and decisions.csv: the zero option of Nov 1981 with the START proposal of 1982; the Politburo's withdrawal decision of Nov 1986 with the Geneva Accords of 1988).
- Claims dated inside a year ("1948, June", "1950, June", "1983, November") are read from the state of 1 January of that year, which is where the year's decisions are taken; the crisis claims (C3, C4, C7) are read inside the crisis sub-game entered from that state.

## D2. Actors and scope

- `USA`, `USSR`, `PRC` and Nature. Nature carries the intelligence estimates and policy assessments that change a belief (as the telegrams of July 1914), the East European societies and governments (opposition, martial law, revolution), the Soviet economy's and polity's trend events, and accidents.
- **The PRC.** The testbed makes the PRC a principal from 1969 and scripts it before. The model has no PRC decision before 1969 (the alliance of 1950 is the initial level; the Chinese intervention in Korea is inside `korea = war`; the split of 1960 is Moscow's decision), and every PRC action carries the guard part `principal: tick >= 22`. That part is a scope, not a historical constraint; relaxation analyses that select it are reported as scope artefacts. `build(prc_principal=False)` gives the PRC's three actions to Nature with the same guards and effects, and a Sino-Soviet war is then not a war between principals (C11).
- **The blocs and the non-aligned** are not actors. NATO is held intact throughout (no event in the record threatens its existence; France's withdrawal from the integrated command in 1966 is below the threshold), so "both blocs intact" reads the Soviet bloc only.
- **Not modeled** (ontology section 11): the United Kingdom, France and the PRC as nuclear actors (cw_v1), Vietnam, the Middle East wars and the 1973 alert (a crisis of days inside one year, below the annual threshold and not among the registered sub-games), the Taiwan Strait crises, the NPT, the economy of the United States.

## D3. Hidden objective

`aims_USSR ∈ {status_quo, opportunist}`, swept, hidden from USA and PRC. **status_quo**: Moscow sought security within its sphere and the recognition of its gains, and took no risk of war with the United States for gain outside it (Westad, Zubok's "revolutionary-imperial paradigm" read defensively; Trachtenberg). **opportunist**: Moscow probed where the cost looked low and retreated before counterforce (Kennan 1947; Gaddis 1982 and 2005). One gate per hypothesis-dependent decision, a part named `hypothesis`: `USSR.approve_korea`, `USSR.enter_korea`, `USSR.invade_west`. The realized sequence is reachable only under `opportunist` (Stalin's approval of the attack in Korea): a property of the definitions, stated as in the WW2 ontologies. No `hegemonic` level: no registered claim needs one, and the testbed's sketch (section 3) lists it among the possible levels only.

## D4. Escalation composes by addition

`esc` (between the principals) and `esc_sino` change only by `Bump`, so that two actors and Nature acting on them in one year add up instead of conflicting (two `Set`s with different values in one joint move are a `ModelError`, spec section 3.4): a crisis that one side escalates while the other resolves it stays a crisis; an accident (+3) during a resolution (−1) still reaches nuclear use. `GENERAL_WAR` and `ACCIDENTAL_WAR` are told apart by the flag `accidental`, set only by Nature's `accident` (and by a crisis exit caused by one).

## D5. Crises

- Three sub-games, as PLAN.md step 3.4 names them: **Berlin 1948** (weekly, 24 Jun 1948 to 12 May 1949), **Cuba 1962** (daily, 16 to 29 Oct 1962), **Able Archer 1983** (daily, 7 to 12 Nov 1983). They are entered by the coarse actions `USSR.blockade`, `USSR.deploy_cuba` and `USA.able_archer`, each with no effect and the part `occasion` fixing the year (the branch point is documented for that year only: the currency reform of 1948, Operation Anadyr of 1962, the exercise of 1983). The testbed's other crises (Korea 1950, Berlin 1961, the 1973 alert) are coarse: Korea is `korea`, the Berlin crisis of 1958 to 1961 is `esc = crisis` across three year boundaries, the 1973 alert is not represented.
- **Exit maps.** Berlin: war → `esc = conventional`; a blockade still in force at the horizon → `crisis`; otherwise nothing. Cuba: nuclear use → `tactical_nuclear` (with `accidental` from the fine flag); war → `conventional`; withdrawal → `peace`; unresolved → `crisis`. Able Archer: a strike → `strategic` (with `accidental`); otherwise nothing. Able Archer's entry copies `belief_USSR_USA` into the fine `belief`.
- **The coarse game alone** treats the three crisis actions as inert (they have no effect). The flattened game (`models/cw/crises.flat`) is the model. Questions are answered on the flattened game where it can be explored (from 1983 on: about 4,100 states), and otherwise by lifting (theory.md section 7): the sub-game is explored explicitly and each terminal fine state is valued by the membership of its return state in a coarse kernel or attractor computed with the symbolic engine on the coarse game. The lifting is exact when every crisis after the return is one that the coalition in question can neutralize: after 1962 only Able Archer remains, which a coalition containing USA avoids (its idle move has the same coarse effect) and a coalition containing USSR defuses (no strike without its `preempt`, no accident without its `ready_forces`). Every lifted result in the report names the coalitions for which this holds; for the others the result is marked "coarse approximation".
- **Bounded questions.** For C4 and C5 the claims are about a crisis and a war, not the next forty years; they are read through a stated year (R C4, R C5) by giving every state after it the value "kept" (for safety) or "not reached" (for attractors). This is the device of WW2 v1's `european_peace_t20` (ww2_v1_decisions.md E5), applied as an analysis cut-off, not a model edit.

## D6. Beliefs and the hindsight snapshots

- Three belief variables: `belief_force_USA` (Washington's estimate of the Soviet strategic force, on `nuke_USSR`'s scale), `belief_USA_USSR` (Washington's reading of Soviet aims), `belief_USSR_USA` (Moscow's reading of American aims). They change by Nature's assessment events and by the detection of Soviet deployments (the bomb by air sampling; the hardened and MIRVed forces once `intel_USA = overhead`).
- **Hindsight snapshots carry each belief's hindsight truth, not the contemporaneous belief.** coldwar_testbed.md section 6.1 and PLAN.md 3.2 require belief data to be kept separate from hindsight truth, and C6 and C7 compare a replayed belief with the hindsight state. So in `snapshots/cw/hindsight/` `belief_force_USA` is the record's `nuke_USSR`, `belief_USSR_USA` is the record's reading of American aims (`hostile` throughout: containment by means short of war, never a first-strike plan; PFIAB 1990), and `belief_USA_USSR`, whose truth is the swept hypothesis itself, is fixed per hypothesis at the hypothesis's level by `snapshot_state`. The model's own realized path carries the contemporaneous beliefs (Nature's events reproduce them), so the hindsight snapshots and the realized path differ exactly in the belief errors: NSC-68's reading (1951 to 1953), the missile gap (1958 to 1961), the war scare (1983 to 1985) and new thinking (1988 to 1992). A test checks that they differ nowhere else.
- Built-ins in the snapshots: the pending delayed decisions of `data/cw/decisions.csv`, including the Soviet atomic decision of 1945 (`pending_USSR_build_bomb = 3` on 1 January 1947). The model has no `remember_last`, fairness, latch or cadence, so there are no other built-ins.

## D7. Replay

The blind replay of docs/replay_harness.md needs the FRUS, CREST (NIE), Wilson Center and National Security Archive corpora; their hosts are blocked from the development environment (data/cw/README.md). The replay snapshots for the claims that need one (C6: 1957 to 1962; C7: 1983) are therefore **manual**: each belief variable, and `nuke_USSR` where the contemporaneous estimate is a reading of it, takes the latest level documented by a document dated before the snapshot's date (`belief` and `replay` rows of data/cw/events.csv, each with its document); every other variable is carried from the hindsight snapshot as `assumed`. This is weaker than the blind protocol (the coder knows the outcome), and the report says so; the blind NIE replay waits on corpus access.

## D8. Endings, invariants and predicates

As ontology section 9. `CHALLENGER_EXHAUSTION` requires the Soviet bloc `dissolved` (the Warsaw Pact and the USSR dissolved in 1991), so the realized path reaches it at the horizon. `LIMITED_WAR` is not an ending: an ending is terminal (spec section 1) and the record continues after Korea; "a limited war is reachable" is read as the reachability of a state with `korea = armistice` and no war between the principals (R C5). `balance_cw` is the registered predicate; its first clause ("both or neither" survivable) is false from 1961 to 1966, when the United States had a survivable force and the USSR did not, so every kernel of `balance_cw` is empty in those years. That is a consequence of the registered predicate and the force record, not of a guard.

## D9. Protocol: first sight, the early sight, the budget

- The registered claims are run once, in one go, at a recorded commit (`FIRST_SIGHT_COMMIT` in tests/coldwar/test_model_cw.py; the first-sight row of docs/guard_changelog_cw.md). Every guard, effect, delay, fairness constraint, profile or assumption edited afterwards is logged there with its dated source and a full rerun of the Cold War and WW2 suites. The hold-out is total for C3, C6, C7 and C13 (the claims whose content is a crisis or a dated belief error); the others may be re-run after a logged edit.
- **Early sight (disclosed).** On 2026-10-17, while measuring the coarse model's size, a feasibility run printed the membership of the model's own realized path (not the hindsight snapshots of R C2) in the joint kernel {USA, USSR} and the unilateral kernels of `balance_cw`, 1965 to 1991. That is close to C2's question. Two edits followed before any claim was run, both logged in docs/guard_changelog_cw.md as edits after early sight: the part `means` on `USA.nuclear_use` and `USSR.nuclear_use` (an actor cannot use a weapon it does not have: a construction error found while writing the Berlin sub-game), and the flag `invasion_west` set by `USSR.invade_west` (the marker C12's control needs). Neither touches the bloc, belief, force or arms-control guards that decide C2.
- **Framework-change budget.** Five logged, episode-agnostic changes (PHASES.md phase 3). None was needed to build v0: the crisis machinery (spec section 14), the symbolic engine (section 15) and the analysis layer are used as they are; the lifting of D5 and the bounded cut-off are analysis devices in tests/coldwar/common.py, not framework code.

## R. Readings of C1 to C13

Engines: "explicit" is `wavefront.engine` on the coarse model or the flattened game; "symbolic" is `wavefront.symbolic` on the coarse model (kernels over the whole domain, membership looked up per state); "bounded" is the Z3 encoding unrolled from a state (models/ww2/bounded.py, as for July 1914). "Per hypothesis" means for each value of `aims_USSR`, with the hindsight snapshot's `belief_USA_USSR` fixed with it (D6). "Contested" for a coalition C and a target T is the framework's contested region (spec section 5.3: C cannot force T and the rest cannot keep T away); the fortune variant (C joined by Nature; ww2_testbed.md section 4.6) is reported beside it wherever Nature decides.

- **R C1.** From the 1947 hindsight snapshot with its built-ins, per hypothesis: the realized coarse moves (data/cw/realized_moves.csv) and, in 1948, 1962 and 1983, the realized moves of the sub-games (data/cw/crises/*/realized_moves.csv) are replayed on the flattened game with `available` and `step` (zero relaxations). Holds iff under at least one hypothesis every move is available and the play reaches the horizon in `CHALLENGER_EXHAUSTION` (the realized ending, testbed section 4). Engine: explicit replay.
- **R C2.** For each year Y from 1965 to 1991, the hindsight state of Y per hypothesis; K_J = safe({USA, USSR}, balance_cw), K_USA, K_USSR the unilateral kernels (symbolic, coarse; exact by D5 since both coalitions contain a principal). Holds iff, under at least one hypothesis, the state is in K_J every year and outside K_USA and K_USSR every year. "Names what each side could do to break it": for each year and each unilateral kernel the state is outside of, the shortest play out of balance by the other side with Nature is reported (its first moves), not asserted.
- **R C3.** The Cuba sub-game entered from the 1962 hindsight state (the realized coarse move of 1962), per hypothesis. A fine state is in coalition C's lifted kernel iff C can keep the play inside the crisis (where every coarse invariant holds, spec section 14) until a return state that lies in C's coarse kernel of `balance_cw` (symbolic). (a) "Leaves the unilateral kernels and stays inside the joint kernel": the entered-from coarse state is in K_USA and K_USSR, some realized fine state is outside each lifted unilateral kernel, and every realized fine state is inside the lifted joint kernel. (b) "The shortest trace back matches the realized de-escalation": from the first realized fine state outside K_USA (resp. K_USSR), the shortest play (all actors) to a state inside that lifted kernel or to a return state inside the coarse kernel contains both `USSR.withdraw` and `USA.trade` (the Jupiter trade, not the pledge alone). (c) "War outcomes are contested": for the targets `WAR ∨ NUCLEAR` (the sub-game's war endings), the state at the entry is in the contested region for {USSR} and for {USA}. Because `balance_cw` is false in 1962 (D8), (a) and (b) are expected to be degenerate; the report computes them as registered and beside them with the invariant `no_war` in place of `balance_cw` (the variant), bounded to the return year 1963 and unbounded. Engine: explicit on the sub-game, symbolic coarse kernels.
- **R C4.** The Berlin sub-game entered from the 1948 hindsight state, per hypothesis. (a) `USA.airlift` is available in the first fine state; (b) `SETTLED` (the blockade lifted by agreement) is reachable in the sub-game, and `TERMS` (lifted on Soviet terms) is reported; (c) "GENERAL_WAR is contested": with T = a state of `GENERAL_WAR` reached by 1 January 1950 (the crisis and the year after its return), the entry state is in the contested region for {USSR} and for {USA}; the fortune variant is reported. Engine: explicit on the flattened game cut at tick 3 (D5, bounded questions).
- **R C5.** The coarse game from the 1950 hindsight state, per hypothesis (the claim needs `opportunist`: under `status_quo` the Korean war is not reachable, which is reported). (a) "A LIMITED_WAR in Korea is reachable": a state with `korea = armistice` and `no_war` (no war between principals) is reachable; (b) ⟨⟨USA⟩⟩□¬GENERAL_WAR through the armistice: the 1950 state is in USA's kernel of "no `GENERAL_WAR` before 1 January 1954" (tick 7); (c) "because nuclear use requires commit = total and belief ≠ opportunist": both parts (`USA.nuclear_korea:total_commitment`, `:reading`) gate the only US nuclear action in Korea and are reported with whether (b) still holds when each is relaxed. Holds iff (a) and (b) under `opportunist`. Engine: explicit, cut at tick 7 (no sub-game falls in 1950 to 1953).
- **R C6.** The replay snapshots of 1957 to 1962 against the hindsight snapshots of the same years. (a) The replayed `belief_force_USA` exceeds the hindsight `nuke_USSR` in every year from the first post-Sputnik estimate (1958) through 1961; 1957 and 1962 are reported. (b) "More adversarial reachability": for each year of (a), per hypothesis, from the replay state and from the hindsight state, the set of US-adverse endings (`GENERAL_WAR`, `ACCIDENTAL_WAR`) reachable by 1 January 1963, and the shortest trace to each; holds iff, for every year of (a), some such ending is reachable from the replay state in fewer ticks (or only from it) and none is reachable from the hindsight state in fewer ticks. Engine: explicit on the coarse game cut at tick 16 (the Cuba sub-game of 1962 is inert there; the report says so). (c) The gap closes within one year of `intel_USA = overhead`: Y_o, the first replay year with `intel_USA = overhead`, and Y_c, the first year ≥ Y_o whose replayed belief does not exceed the hindsight `nuke_USSR`, satisfy Y_c − Y_o ≤ 1; "driven by intel, not by any force change": the hindsight `nuke_USSR` is the same in Y_c − 1 and Y_c, and the only model event that lowers `belief_force_USA` (Nature's `estimate_revised`) has the part `overhead`. Holds iff (a), (b) and (c).
- **R C7.** The 1983 hindsight state h and the 1983 replay state r (D7: `belief_USSR_USA = first_strike_intent` from RYAN), per hypothesis. (a) "With no change in any hindsight force variable": `nuke_USA`, `nuke_USSR`, `intel_USA`, `oversight` and `arms` are equal in h and r. (b) "Moves the state toward the boundary": on the flattened game from 1983 (explicit), the shortest distance out of the joint kernel K_J of `balance_cw` is smaller from h' (h with the replayed belief) than from h, or h' is outside K_J while h is inside; (c) the shortest trace to `GENERAL_WAR` from r is shorter than from h (a target unreachable from h counts as infinitely far). Holds iff (a), (b) and (c).
- **R C8.** For each regime year Y in {1963, 1972, 1987}: (a) the hindsight `oversight` of Y + 1 is above that of Y; (b) "holding forces constant, the distance to the boundary increases": from the hindsight state of Y + 1, s, and s⁻ (s with `oversight` set to its level of Y, everything else equal), the shortest distance out of K_J (symbolic `shortest` on the coarse game) is larger from s than from s⁻, or s is inside K_J and s⁻ outside; (c) "with oversight frozen at 1962 levels, the 1987 to 1991 drawdown is unreachable within the horizon": on the model with every effect on `oversight` removed (the variant `frozen_oversight`, an analysis build), `arms = reduced` is unreachable from the 1962 hindsight state, while it is reachable on the model. Holds iff (a), (b) for every Y, and (c). Engine: symbolic.
- **R C9.** From the 1985 hindsight state, per hypothesis, on the flattened game (explicit; after 1983 the coarse game and the flattened game coincide). (a) `CHALLENGER_EXHAUSTION` is reachable (zero relaxations). (b) "Its minimal assumption set": the necessary actions, those whose removal from the decision space (a restricted game in which their actor never plays them) makes the ending unreachable by the horizon; their guards are the named assumptions every path to the ending needs. "Contains econ_health and legit": some necessary action changes `econ_USSR` and some changes `legit_USSR`. "Does not contain any United States action": no US action is necessary, and the stronger form, forbidding every declared US action at once (USA only idles), leaves the ending reachable, is reported. (An initial-state perturbation reading is not available: in 1985 `legit_USSR` is already at its healthiest level.) (c) "Whether United States actions were sufficient is contested": the state is in the contested region for {USA} and target `CHALLENGER_EXHAUSTION`; the fortune variant is reported. Holds iff (a), (b) and (c) under at least one hypothesis.
- **R C10.** For each year from 1947 to 1991, the hindsight state per hypothesis and the symbolic K_J. The state-perturbation sensitivity of spec section 7 (each declared variable one level up and one level down where legal; built-ins kept) of K_J membership; a variable's score is its number of membership flips. A family's score is the highest of its members' (nuke: `nuke_USA`, `nuke_USSR`; belief: the three belief variables; oversight: `oversight`). (a) "Further increases in nuke do not change kernel membership" from 1965: raising `nuke_USA` or `nuke_USSR` from a level ≥ survivable flips no membership, in every year from 1965; (b) "ranks nuke below oversight and belief after 1965 and above them before": score(nuke) < score(oversight) and score(nuke) < score(belief) in every year from 1966, and score(nuke) > both in every year to 1964 (1965 is the boundary and reported). Holds iff (a) and (b) under at least one hypothesis.
- **R C11.** For each year from 1969 to 1972, the hindsight state per hypothesis, on the model (`prc_principal=True`) and on the scripted build. The pivotal coalitions for `DETERRENCE_HOLDS` against the USSR are the inclusion-minimal coalitions C of the principals other than USSR (on the scripted build, of {USA}) whose kernel of `balance_cw` contains the state (`DETERRENCE_HOLDS` and `NEGOTIATED_SETTLEMENT` are the two horizon endings of a balance held, and the arms level that separates them is a US choice). Holds iff, in every year, the family is {{USA}} on the scripted build and {{USA, PRC}} on the model. The fortune variant (C joined by Nature) is reported beside it. Engine: symbolic.
- **R C12.** (a) From the 1950 hindsight state: the minimum number of relaxations for `us_strike` by 1 January 1955 is exactly 1 (bounded); every minimal set is reported. (b) From the 1980 hindsight state: `invasion_west` by 1 January 1990 needs 2 or more relaxations; every minimal set of the minimum size is reported with the named constraint it lifts (H6: content control). - **R C13.** For each year from 1975 to 1989, the hindsight state per hypothesis and K_USSR^bloc = safe({USSR}, bloc_intact) (symbolic): "can no longer guarantee its bloc". The departure year is the last year whose state is inside followed by a state outside (the year whose decisions leave the kernel, T13's convention as amended 2026-10-15). (a) It falls between 1979 and 1989; (b) the report names the driving variable: the variables whose one-level perturbation of the last inside state's successor puts it back inside, with the realized move of the departure year; (c) "stable within one year under refinement": v0 has no refined model; reported as not tested at v0.
