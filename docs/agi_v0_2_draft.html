AGI v0.2 (draft): what open weights does

v0 and v0.1 give an open-weight release one effect: oversight drops a level. That cannot answer whether open weights help or hurt. This draft proposes adding the effects the question is really about, each as a switch, so the model can compare the world with and without them.

Draft. No guard, model file, claim or job exists for v0.2 and nothing has been run. Under the testbed rules an edit to actions is a new model version with its own registered claims; v0 and v0.1 results stand as computed.

1 · Why

The owner's question of 17 November: within the model, does an open-weight release do more good or more harm, and can something like "it pushes the labs to deploy faster" be inferred?

  • It cannot be read out today. L.open_weights only lowers oversight by one level. The sign is written in, not found.
  • The existing results barely lean on it. A diagnostic of 17 November on v0.1 (not a registered claim): forbidding open_weights changes no shortest play, because race lowers oversight too. Forbidding both removes the slide into disempowerment within 24 quarters. The silent takeover is unchanged either way (10 and 7 quarters).
  • Its one distinct role (from the guards, not run): it is the labs' only way to lower oversight during a pause, since a pause blocks racing and not releasing.

A warning about the design itself. If v0.2 adds only harmful effects, "open weights are harmful" is again an input. To let the model say anything about the balance, at least one beneficial channel has to be in it. That is why channel C below is proposed even though it has the thinnest basis.

2 · The channels

Expected to hurt · proposed first

A · No recall

Once weights are public, a government halt acts on the labs' hosted service and not on the copies.

Proposed rule
A permanent flag weights_open. While it is set, a pause_mandate no longer rolls dependence back and no longer cancels a pending adoption wave.
Option, for the owner
Also: a shutdown_attempt cannot succeed, and comply cannot put the AI back under supervision. Stronger, and with less basis.
Basis
June 2026: the Fable 5 suspension took effect within hours because access was hosted (verified). DeepSeek-R1 spread within a week of release and was not recalled (dates from memory, to verify).
Confidence
Medium for the pause rule; low for the shutdown option.
Expected to hurt · the owner's hypothesis

B · Race pressure

A public release at the frontier pushes the leading labs to ship faster to keep their lead.

Proposed rule
After a release, the labs must play deploy_agents, scale or race within 2 quarters whenever one is available (a fairness constraint on L). They can no longer simply wait.
Who releases
The labs are one player, so "a rival's release pressures the leaders" has no rival to come from. Two readings: the labs' own action (as now), or a chance event (Nature.open_release: someone outside the frontier labs releases). The second fits the hypothesis better.
Basis
Not sourced yet. Candidates to check: the frontier labs' releases and price cuts in the weeks after DeepSeek-R1 (January 2025). Until sourced it is speculative=True by the project rule, and every result that depends on it is flagged.
Note
This is an assumption put in. The model then shows what follows from it; it cannot confirm the pressure exists.
Expected to help · needed for a fair comparison

C · Outside testing

With the weights public, independent testers get the access to internals that the model's own definition of strong oversight asks for.

Proposed rule
While weights_open is set, fund_oversight has a delay of 1 quarter instead of 3, and detection of covert acts also works at oversight = none.
What it does not give
The power to block a deployment, the other half of strong oversight. So it speeds oversight up; it does not grant it.
Basis
Not sourced yet. Candidates: published interpretability and red-team work on open-weight models. speculative=True until sourced.
Not in v0.2

D · Diffusion

A second AI system running outside the labs' control. This is the largest effect people argue about, and it needs the second system (A2) planned for model v1 and claim G22.

Consequence
v0.2 can speak to recall, pressure and testing. It cannot speak to proliferation, and the report must say so on its front page.

3 · How the change is built

  • One new flag, three switches. weights_open is added once. Channels A, B and C are separate build options, each on or off, run beside the baseline as v0's sweeps are. A difference between two builds then has one cause.
  • All switches off must reproduce v0.1 exactly. That is the first registered claim (E1), so a wiring mistake is caught before anything is interpreted.
  • A separate package (models/agi_v0_2/), as v0.1, so the stored results of v0 and v0.1 stay readable. No framework change is expected: a flag, one fairness constraint, guard conditions.
  • The existing effect stays (oversight −1), and open_weights stays repeatable.

5 · Cost

  • Bounded claims (E1, E2, E3 (a)): minutes each on the current 30 GB.
  • Kernels (E3 (b), E4, E5): a v0.1 kernel is about 1 hour and 10 GB. The new flag roughly doubles the state space and B's counter adds a factor of about three; how that turns into time and memory is not predictable from v0.1's figures (its one counter cost 7 times the time and 1.8 times the memory). A timing probe comes first, as for v0.1.
  • Builds: E5 needs four kernels on two builds; E3 and E4 one or two each. About 8 to 10 kernel runs in all, which is a day on the 128 GB machine if each fits in about 20 GB.

6 · What the owner decides

  1. Which channels. Proposed: A, B and C, each as a switch. (A and B alone would fix the sign in advance.)
  2. Channel A's reach. The pause rule only (proposed), or also a failed shutdown and no return to supervision.
  3. Channel B's reading. The release as a chance event from outside the frontier labs (proposed), or the labs' own action. And the window: 2 quarters (proposed).
  4. Sources. Whether to proceed with B and C as speculative if no dated source is found, with every dependent result flagged.
  5. The treaty delay. Part A showed that D4's result rests on treaties having no delay; the research gives 2 quarters. Proposed: a separate version (v0.3), so that v0.2's differences are about open weights only. It can instead be a fourth switch in v0.2 at the cost of one more counter in every kernel.
  6. The predictions of section 4.
