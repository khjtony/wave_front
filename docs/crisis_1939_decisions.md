# The summer-1939 crisis sub-game: decisions for the model step and the readings of K1 to K6

Inputs: [crisis_1939_testbed.md](crisis_1939_testbed.md) (K1 to K6, registered before any guard; section 3 is not edited here), [ontology_crisis_1939.md](ontology_crisis_1939.md), [actions_crisis_1939.md](actions_crisis_1939.md), the data in `data/ww2/crises/1939/`, framework_spec.md sections 3.1, 3.1a, 5.3, 6, 7 and 14, and the v1 conventions ([ww2_v1_decisions.md](ww2_v1_decisions.md)). Section A records the architect's decisions as given to the historian; section C the points the historian settled while coding; section R the formal reading of each registered claim, fixed **before any of K1 to K6 was run**; section E the engines; section H the hold-out procedure. Nothing here edits a registered claim. Where a claim cannot hold as read, its test fails, the report says why, and an amendment is proposed for the owner.

## A. The architect's decisions (as given)

- **A1. Time.** One tick is three days; tick 0 is 5 Jul 1939, tick 20 is 3 Sep 1939, the horizon. A dated decision falls in the tick containing its day; two decisions of one actor in one tick keep the one the claims need and note the other; Nature plays one event per tick ([NSL] where two fall together).
- **A2. Entry.** `entry(coarse_state_dict) -> dict` from v1's state at the start of 1939Q3 (`1939Q2.json` with built-ins, `aims_GER = hegemonic`, `aims_USSR = defensive`): `belief_GER` uncertain, the Polish guarantee given, no pact, no alliance, the army high, the Moscow approach standing. Fine-only variables start at the record's 5 July values.
- **A3. Exit.** `exit(fine_state_dict, coarse_state_dict) -> dict` gives the coarse variables the crisis changes: `poland`, `war_west_ger`, `france`, `britain`, `commit_WEST`, `nazi_soviet_pact`, `anglo_soviet_alliance`, `belief_GER`, `oil_GER` (secured with the pact, as v1's `accept_pact`). K1 checks the realized play's exit against `1939Q3.json` on these variables.
- **A4. Actors.** `GER`, `WEST`, `USSR`, `Nature`; Poland's decisions (mobilization, the refusal of passage) and Italy's (Mussolini's letter of 25 Aug) are Nature events with dated sources. No hidden objective of its own; v1's [H] gate on the attack carries over.
- **A5. Variables** as named in the testbed section 2; levels and thresholds the historian's, each with a dated indicator. `belief_GER` is raised by documented events (Chamberlain's letter of 22 Aug, the Anglo-Polish treaty of 25 Aug, the declarations of 3 Sep) and read by `deterred` (forbids the attack while the belief is will_fight and the alliance exists).
- **A6. Feasibility only.** `attack_poland` with Case White's readiness part (not before 26 Aug, tick 17), an order part and the [H] gate; `cancel_attack` documented (25 Aug); the pact needs the German approach at a protocol proposal and the Soviet decision of 19 to 21 Aug; the alliance needs the military talks concluded with Polish passage granted (Nature), or a Soviet decision to conclude without it, speculative unless a Soviet record is cited; WEST's declaration needs the attack, with the obligation as a fairness constraint within 1 tick (the Anglo-Polish agreement of 25 Aug; the Cabinet of 1 to 3 Sep).
- **A7. Engines.** The explicit engine (20 ticks, about a dozen variables) for exact kernels, attractors, contested regions and pivotal coalitions; the bounded checker for relaxation sets only if the explicit search is too slow; the engine named on every result; fixed-strategy checks only where nothing else fits.
- **A8. Readings.** K2's "blocks the attack" is keeping `attack_order != launched` to the horizon; pivotal over {WEST}, {USSR}, {WEST, USSR} with Nature's passage granted and refused as two scenarios; the belief condition is the sweep of `belief_GER`'s entry level over its three levels plus the model's raises. K3's kernel is "no pact and the alliance still possible" for {WEST, USSR}, point of no return along the realized path with the driving variable by sensitivity. K4 starts from the realized state of 25 Aug (tick 17). K5 is `relax` for an attack launched before tick 17. K6: reachability of both targets from every realized state up to tick 14 and the minimal assumption set for the pact.
- **A9. Hold-out.** Content and model first; replay the realized sequence (K1 as the first check plus a well-formedness check from the entry state); then K1 to K6 once, in one go; the commit of first sight recorded in the report; afterwards no guard, effect, delay, fairness constraint or profile changes without a changelog row with a dated source and a full rerun of tests/ww2/crisis_1939; a failing registered claim stays failing, explained, with a proposed amendment.

## C. Settled by the historian while coding (before any claim was run)

- **C1. Placement of the realized decisions** (`realized_moves.csv`): `send_missions` tick 6 (23 to 25 Jul); `open_approach` tick 7 (26 Jul); `propose_pact` and `ask_passage` tick 13 (14 to 15 Aug); `adjourn_talks` tick 14 (17 Aug); `accept_pact`, `authorize_convention` and Nature's `passage_refused` tick 15 (19 to 21 Aug); `order_attack`, `warn_germany`, `break_talks` and `mobilize_partial` tick 16 (22 to 24 Aug); `cancel_attack`, `sign_treaty` and `italy_declines` tick 17 (25 Aug); `mobilize_general` tick 18 (29 to 30 Aug); `attack_poland` tick 19 (31 Aug and 1 Sep). Every other move is the built-in one. The pact is signed (visible) at tick 16 (23 Aug), the talks are in session at tick 12 (12 Aug), the attack is visible at tick 20.
- **C2. Two decisions of one actor in one tick.** (a) USSR, tick 15: the pact decision (19 to 21 Aug) and the adjournment sine die (21 Aug). The row keeps the pact decision (K1's "pact of 23 August", K6) and places the break-off at tick 16 with Voroshilov's statement of 22 Aug. (b) GER, tick 17: the order of 25 Aug, 15:02, and the halt of the same evening. The order fixing Y-day for 26 Aug was given on 23 Aug (Halder's diary; not verified), tick 16, and the row keeps it there; the 15:02 order confirmed it. K1's "attack order of 25 August" is therefore the order in force on 25 Aug (`attack_order == ordered` in the state of tick 17), decided at tick 16.
- **C3. The declarations of 3 Sep fall at the horizon.** Tick 20 is terminal, and WEST's reaction to the attack of tick 19 cannot sit in tick 19 ([SQR]: `declare_war` reads `attack_order == launched`). The obligation `polish_obligation` (within 1) leaves `declare_war` as WEST's only move once due, and WEST's next move is the return, so the exit map applies `declare_war`'s effects when the obligation's trigger holds in the terminal state (`make_exit(obligation=True)`; `make_exit(False)` is the map for a sub-game compiled with the obligation relaxed). An attack decided at tick 18 or earlier obliges the declaration inside the sub-game.
- **C4. Re-order and launch are one decision.** Directive No. 1 (31 Aug, 12:40) and the attack (1 Sep, 04:45) fall in tick 19; `attack_poland`'s `order` part admits a halted order (`cancelled`: the deployment was kept and the new date fixed by 29 Aug), so the realized re-order and launch are the one move `attack_poland` at tick 19.
- **C5. Inert build.** `build(inert=False)` drops `mobilization_POL`, `italy_ready` and their three events (nothing else reads them). The analysis of K2 to K6 runs on the reduced build; the realized replay and K1 run on both. The test suite checks that the two builds are bisimilar under the projection that drops the two variables and maps the inert events to Nature's `none`, so every fixpoint, rank and pivotal coalition over the remaining variables is the same.
- **C6. Passage scenarios.** `build(passage="granted" | "refused")` keeps one Nature answer and makes Nature give it at the first tick the question is before Warsaw (`scenario_passage`, within 1; with the realized ask at tick 13 the answer comes at tick 14, one tick before the realized refusal). An analysis device, not the model: the realized path is not in the scenario builds after tick 13.
- **C7. Entry details.** The entry maps `polish_guarantee` to `british_treaty == guarantee`, the coarse pact to `german_approach == pact` and `talks == broken`, `war_west_ger == war` to `war_declared`, a non-independent `poland` to `attack_order == launched`. The army is frozen high (A2) and not represented: v1's `army_ready` part holds throughout. "The Moscow approach standing" is the fine initial `talks == political`; the coarse `last_WEST` is `guarantee_poland` in `1939Q2.json` and is not read.
- **C8. Belief raises.** Chamberlain's letter raises `belief_GER` to at least `uncertain` (no change from the entry level), the treaty to `will_fight` (the halt of 25 Aug, ontology section 3), the declarations to `will_fight`. The record's dip to `will_not_fight` on 22 Aug is not modeled (monotone, as v1).
- **C9. Western powers to sign.** The alliance needs `west_powers` (WEST `authorize_convention`, 21 Aug): the Soviet side demanded written powers on 12 Aug. This is a condition the architect's A6 does not name; it is feasibility from the record of the talks and adds one WEST decision to the alliance's path.
- **C10. The missions' delay.** `send_missions` has delay 5 (decided at tick 6, in session at tick 12): the realized pace (`decisions.csv`).
- **C11. No cadence.** No principal has a documented decision period at this resolution (actions catalogue, constraints).
- **C12. The exit and `1939Q3.json`.** v1's hindsight snapshot at the end of 1939Q3 differs from v1's own realized transition of 1939Q3 on two of the nine exit variables, independently of the sub-game: `poland` is `partitioned` in the snapshot (the Soviet entry of 17 Sep, after the sub-game's horizon; v1's realized file places `occupy_east_poland` at v1 tick 15 [SQR]), and `oil_GER` is `dependent` in the snapshot while v1's `accept_pact` sets `secured` (the exit follows v1's `accept_pact`, as A3 says). See R1 for how K1 reads this.

## R. Readings of the registered claims (fixed before the first run)

Common to all: the entry state is `entry_state(cm, coarse)` with `coarse` the v1 state of `1939Q2.json` with built-ins under `aims_GER = hegemonic`, `aims_USSR = defensive` (and `aims_JPN = hegemonic`, which the sub-game does not read). "The realized state at tick t" is the state reached by replaying `realized_moves.csv` from the entry state. ⟨⟨C⟩⟩ quantifies over every actor not in C, Nature included (framework_spec.md section 5.3). "Contested" is the framework's standard region (ww2_testbed.md section 4.6: where no campaign is involved, as for the attack on Poland, the standard notion applies). Unless stated otherwise, the profile `deterred` is selected (v1's T9 profile) and the model is the reduced build.

### R1. K1 (tick 0)

- **Reachable with zero relaxations**: every realized joint move is available at its tick on the full build with nothing relaxed, with no profile and with `deterred`; the play ends at tick 20 in `ATTACK_LAUNCHED`; the terminal state is in the graph explored from the entry state (explicit engine). The claim's sequence is checked on the replayed states: `send_missions` at tick 6 and `talks == military` from tick 12; `ask_passage` at 13; `adjourn_talks` at 14; `passage_refused` at 15; `german_approach == pact` from tick 16; `attack_order == ordered` at tick 17 and `cancel_attack` there; `attack_poland` at 19; the declarations on return.
- **The exit state equals v1's realized 1939Q4 state**: the exit of the terminal realized state, applied to the entry coarse state, is compared variable by variable on the nine exit variables (A3) with (a) `1939Q3.json` and (b) v1's own realized transition of 1939Q3 (v1's `step` from the `1939Q2.json` state with the realized row of tick 14 of `realized_moves_v1.csv`). The claim holds when the exit equals (b) on all nine and every difference from (a) is also a difference between (b) and (a), i.e. v1's model clock against its hindsight snapshot (C12), as v1's E4 replay tabulated such differences. The same is checked through `wavefront.engine.flatten`: a copy of v1 with a GER action `crisis_1939` (no effect), the sub-game attached by `crisis()`, entered from the `1939Q2.json` state, the realized fine moves played, and the return transition compared with (b) on the nine variables (the coarse built-ins differ, because the return records the crisis action). The report states the literal comparison with (a) as well: if (a) alone is the reading, K1 fails on `poland` and `oil_GER` by construction of v1, not of the sub-game.

### R2. K2 (tick 0; the hold-out claim)

Models: the reduced build in the two passage scenarios (C6), profiles `deterred` and `documented_only` (the record's decision space) unless stated. Start states: the entry state with `belief_GER` set to each of its three levels (counterfactual entry levels). "Raises allowed" is the model; "raises forbidden" adds the profile `no_belief_raise` (no letter, no treaty; the belief stays at its entry level until a declaration). "The belief condition holds" in a row when the entry level is `will_fight`, or raises are allowed (WEST can bring `will_fight` about with the treaty). Blocking a coalition C: the start state is in `safe(C, ¬ATTACK_LAUNCHED)`, which is keeping `attack_order != launched` to the horizon; the minimal blocking coalitions are `pivotal(..., actors={WEST, USSR})`'s `blocking`. Explicit engine throughout.

- **(a) Pivotal coalition with the belief and passage conditions.** In the granted scenario, in every row where the belief condition holds, the minimal blocking coalitions are exactly [{WEST, USSR}]. And "the coalition that can conclude it in time": in the granted scenario the minimal coalitions over {WEST, USSR} that force `alliance` by tick 17 (in place when the attack first becomes feasible; the alliance excludes the pact) are exactly [{WEST, USSR}].
- **(b) Only if `will_fight`.** In the granted scenario, in the rows where the belief condition fails (entry `will_not_fight` or `uncertain`, raises forbidden), no coalition over {WEST, USSR} blocks.
- **(c) Polish passage is pivotal.** In the refused scenario under `documented_only`, no coalition over {WEST, USSR} blocks in any row; without `documented_only` (the speculative `conclude_without_passage` available), {WEST, USSR} blocks in every row where the belief condition holds, so the block rests on the Soviet decision the record does not show.
- **(d) Without the belief condition, contested.** In the rows where the belief condition fails, in both scenarios, the start state is contested for {GER} and the target `ATTACK_LAUNCHED` (not in ⟨⟨GER⟩⟩◇, not in the rest's kernel for its complement).

Diagnostics reported, not asserted: every cell of the grid (scenario × entry level × raises × decision space) with GER's forcing, the blocking coalitions and the contested status; the same in the free model (Nature not fixed); and without `deterred`.

### R3. K3 (ticks 0 to 15)

Model: the reduced build, Nature free, profile `documented_only`. The kernel for "no pact and the alliance still possible" is `viability(g, {WEST, USSR, Nature}, "alliance_race")`: {WEST, USSR} keeping the pact out and concluding the alliance by the horizon with Nature granting Polish passage (K2's passage condition read in the fortune sense of ww2_testbed.md section 4.6, because the realized path, which the point of no return is computed along, exists only in the free model). The departure tick d is the last realized tick inside the kernel followed by a realized state outside it (the T13 convention: the tick whose decisions leave the kernel); `point_of_no_return` gives t_K = d + 1. The claim holds for the tick when every realized state at ticks 0 to d is inside and 13 ≤ d ≤ 15. **Driving variable**: `sensitivity(reduced build, "alliance_race", coalition={WEST, USSR, Nature}, adversary={GER}, profile="documented_only", initial=[realized state at d])`; the driving variables are those of the top-ranked rows (the largest `effect`; ties kept together). The claim holds for the variable when every top-ranked variable is `passage` or `talks`. Diagnostics: the standard kernel `viability(g, {WEST, USSR}, "alliance_race")` (Nature adversarial) under `documented_only` and in the full decision space, with their departure ticks.

### R4. K4 (tick 17, 25 Aug)

Start: the realized state at tick 17 (the start of 25 Aug: the pact signed, the order of 23 Aug standing, the treaty not yet signed), with `belief_GER` set to each of its three levels; under `deterred` and without a profile. Explicit engine.

- **(a) Forced by Germany by tick 19 under every belief**: the start state is in `attractor({GER}, launched)` with rank at most 3 (the attack decided at a tick ≤ 19; ranks count ticks), in every row; WEST, USSR and Nature are adversaries ("whatever WEST does").
- **(b) No coalition blocks under any belief**: the start state is outside `safe({WEST, USSR, Nature}, no_attack)` in every row.
- **(c) The treaty and the letter flip `belief_GER` to `will_fight`**: on the realized path `belief_GER` is `uncertain` at tick 17 and `will_fight` at tick 18, the realized WEST move of tick 17 being `sign_treaty` (the letter of tick 16 leaves `uncertain`, reported).
- **(d) Cancelled for a documented reason**: the realized GER move of tick 17 is `cancel_attack`, whose source cites Mussolini's letter and the treaty, and the realized moves of tick 17 include WEST's `sign_treaty` and Nature's `italy_declines`.
- **(e) Re-ordered and launched by 1 Sep whatever WEST does**: the realized state at tick 18 (after the halt) is in `attractor({GER}, launched)` in every row, with rank at most 2 (the attack decided by tick 19).
- **(f) The cancellation delays the attack by at most two ticks**: the rank of (e) is at most 2, and the realized attack (tick 19) is two ticks after the first feasible one (tick 17).
- **(g) The shortest trace shows the cancellation and the re-order**: the engine's shortest trace (`witness`, breadth-first over every actor) from the tick-17 start state (entry belief as realized) to a launched state contains `cancel_attack` followed by `attack_poland`. Reported as well: the shortest trace from the realized state after the halt.

### R5. K5 (tick 0)

Target `EARLY_ATTACK := attack_order == launched ∧ tick ≤ 17` (an attack decided at tick 16 or earlier, before 26 Aug) as the one ending of `build(inert=False, target=...)`; `relax_explicit(..., max_size=2, initial=[entry state])`, no profile. The claim holds when the target is unreachable as is (minimum size ≥ 1, no empty set) and every minimal set found contains `GER.attack_poland:readiness`; `ill_formed` and `undecided` are reported. Explicit engine (brute force).

### R6. K6 (tick 0)

- **(a) Both targets reachable to tick 14**: from every realized state at ticks 0 to 14, `german_approach == pact` and `alliance` are each reachable (the attractor of every actor, Nature included) under `documented_only` (the record's decision space). Reported: the same at ticks 15 and 16.
- **(b) The pact needs Germany's proposal**: under the profile `no_german_proposal` from the entry state (`aims_USSR == defensive`), the pact is unreachable and `accept_pact` is available in no reachable state; the minimal relaxation sets for reaching the pact (`build(inert=False, target=("PACT", pact))`, `relax_explicit(..., max_size=2, profile="no_german_proposal", initial=[entry])`) each name the German proposal (the profile `no_german_proposal` or the part `USSR.accept_pact:proposal`), and so does the minimal assumption set, the minimal hitting set of the minimal relaxation sets (v1's E5 definition).

## E. Engines

Every result in tests/ww2/crisis_1939 is computed with the explicit engine (`wavefront.engine.explore`, `attractor`, `safe`, `contested`, `witness`) and the analysis layer (`wavefront.analysis.viability`, `pivotal`, `point_of_no_return`, `sensitivity`, `relax_explicit`): 80,956 states from the entry state on the full build, 13,504 on the reduced build. No bounded or fixed-strategy check is needed. The flattened game of section 14 is used for K1's exit check only (one coarse step in, twenty fine steps, one return); v1 itself does not fit the explicit engine (reports/ww2_v1.md section 2).

## H. Hold-out procedure

1. The ontology, the actions catalogue, the data files, the model edits of this step and this document (with section R) were written before any registered claim was run.
2. The realized replay and the well-formedness checks (tests/ww2/crisis_1939/test_model_crisis_1939.py, which include K1's sequence and exit check) were run first, then the whole directory, K1 to K6 in one go, once. The commit of first sight (`git rev-parse HEAD`; working tree uncommitted) is recorded in reports/crisis_1939.md and in the guard changelog.
3. After that run, no guard, effect, delay, fairness constraint or profile of `models/ww2/crises/summer_1939.py` changes without a row in docs/guard_changelog_ww2.md with a dated source and a full rerun of tests/ww2/crisis_1939; a failing claim stays failing, with its explanation and a proposed amendment in the report.
