## D1. The PRC a principal from 1950 (decision 3a)

- The scope part `principal` of every PRC action is `tick >= 3` (1950). The action set is the minimal one of prc_experiment.md section 7: `PRC.intervene_korea` (new) and v0's `border_clash`, `rapprochement`, `normalize`. The episodes the research note lists beyond these (the Strait crises, the bomb programme, the polemic of 1959 to 1960, Vietnam 1965, the independent line of 1982) are out of scope; the experiment found them indifferent in every measure on v0's decision space, and the registration excludes them (section 9.1).
- `esc_usprc` (peace, war) is the US-PRC war. `PRC.intervene_korea` (Mao's telegrams of 2 and 13 Oct 1950; alternative: the Russian text of 2 Oct and the Politburo majority of 4 to 5 Oct) sets it to war; `USA.armistice` ends it (the CPV signed the armistice). No crisis level: no action in scope sets one.
- **The link to the US nuclear options in Korea:** `USA.nuclear_korea:total_commitment` is `esc >= conventional ∨ esc_usprc = war` (Truman's press conference of 30 Nov 1950 answered the Chinese offensive; the NSC of Feb and May 1953 concerned the war with the Chinese armies). The part `reading` (`belief_USA_USSR ≠ opportunist`) is v0's.
- **The PRC-to-Nature build** (`build(prc_principal=False)`): Nature carries `intervene_korea`, `border_clash` (and so enters the 1969 sub-game), `rapprochement`, `normalize`, and the sub-game's `accept_talks`; a Sino-Soviet war and a US-PRC war are not wars between principals (`no_war`, `general_war` as v0's scripted build).

## D3. The C5 route (decision 5a)

The route of reports/cw_v0.md section 20 used two facts of v0, which the registration (section 9.1) asks v1 to address on the record:

1. **The Soviet ICBM decision has a dated occasion.** `USSR.icbm_buildup` gains the part `technology: tick >= 1962`: a survivable force needed the silo-based third-generation ICBMs, decreed on 16 Apr 1962 (R-36) and 30 Mar 1963 (UR-100) (Podvig 2001; Zaloga 2002; the decree dates not verified); the first generation was soft and slow to launch. The realized decision (1964) is available; one in 1950 is not. `USSR.mirv` (from `survivable`) is not dated: after this edit it cannot be decided before 1965, eight years earlier than its recorded decision at most; this is noted and not changed (no claim reads the MIRV year closely).
2. **How escalations compose across crises (D4 of v0, revised).** A move that opens combat raises `esc` to `conventional` (`esc := conventional if esc < conventional`) instead of adding one or two rungs: `USA.escalate`, `USSR.escalate`, `USSR.enter_korea` (v0: +2), `USSR.invade_west`. Combat between the principals' forces is the `conventional` rung (ontology section 5); the nuclear rungs are reached only by a nuclear decision (`*.nuclear_use`, `*.preempt`, `USA.nuclear_korea`, `USA.preventive_strike`) or an accident (Kahn, *On Escalation*, 1965: rungs are thresholds each crossed by a decision). Within one standing crisis, moves still compose (a `Set` and a `Bump` in one joint move: `esc := conventional` then −1): an escalation against a resolution leaves the crisis standing, as v0's D4 intended. What v0 allowed and v1 does not: open entry into Korea from a standing Berlin crisis reaching `tactical_nuclear`, and both sides escalating one standing crisis in one year reaching `tactical_nuclear`, each without any nuclear decision.

`USSR.berlin_ultimatum` keeps v0's guard (no date): the route's other undated move is reported by V4 (d), not closed here.

Choices, each a reading of the sources in the actions' `source=`:

- **The strike's conditions** (`USSR.strike`): `sounded` (Moscow asked Washington's reaction first, EBB 49), `refusal` (the threat served to bring Beijing to talks; Gerson 2010), `acquiescence` (the reaction asked for was American indifference; Kissinger 1979; Goldstein 2001). The literature is divided on which condition bound; the guard carries both readings as separate parts, so that a relaxation analysis can name either.
- **The American signal** (`USA.signal`) answers the Soviet escalation of 13 Aug or the soundings (part `occasion`); before either, the record has no American statement on a Sino-Soviet war.
- **Talks** need a Soviet proposal and Beijing's acceptance (Kosygin's request and Zhou's acceptance, 10 to 11 Sep; the statement of 29 Mar).
- **The talks are not carried to the coarse game:** the exit sets `esc_sino = clash` whether or not talks were agreed, so v0's coarse `USSR.strike_china` stays open in 1970 to 1972 until the alignment. Reason: the record keeps the armed confrontation along the border to 1989 (v0's level), and the research note (1.12) suggests, but does not establish, that the meeting removed the coarse strike option. This is a content choice the owner may want to review before first sight (section O).
- **1973 entry by the USSR:** the crisis is the one Brezhnev's message opened (the registration's entry state); the October War itself is not a decision of a principal, and Nature does not enter the crisis.
- **The naval incident** needs the American alert: the Soviet squadron was at readiness from the war's first days (a fact of the entry), so the alert is what put both fleets at raised readiness in contact (Sagan 1985). Grechko's mobilization proposal of 25 Oct (rejected; Israelyan 1995) changes no variable of the sub-game and is not represented.
- `us_began_war` is not set by the 1973 exit: neither the American opposition to a Soviet landing nor an incident at sea is the war begun by NATO of the Warsaw Pact plans.

## D7. Engines

- **Explicit** (`wavefront.engine`) on the flattened game (spec section 14), with the analysis cut-off of v0's D5 (`Cut`: coarse states at the cut are terminal, ending `CUT`) and restricted games (`Forbid`).
- **Symbolic** (`wavefront.symbolic`) on the coarse game, where the crisis actions are inert. The symbolic engine does not encode the flattened game (spec section 15).
- **The composite engine (exact).** No crisis action is available after 1983 (every crisis action has an `occasion` fixing its year). So the symbolic coarse kernel or attractor computed over the whole domain is exact on every state at tick 37 (1 January 1984) or later. For a start state before 1984, the flattened game is explored explicitly from it with the cut at tick 37, and each cut state takes its value from the symbolic result (membership in the kernel, or in the attractor; for a distance, its symbolic `shortest` added to the explicit distance to it). This is exact: the explicit part contains every crisis, and the symbolic part contains none. `tests/coldwar_v1/common.py` has the device (`boundary_kernel`). Its cost is the explicit graph to 1984 (section S): within budget from 1972 on, not from 1967 to 1970 (section S).
- **Layered lifting (exact; for the years the composite engine cannot hold).** The symbolic engine cannot hold the flattened game, and changing it is a framework change (not made; section O). But every crisis action has a one-year occasion, so the crises sit at five known ticks, and every entry map is constant except Able Archer's (which copies one belief variable). The kernel can therefore be computed backward one tick layer at a time on the coarse encoding, with the crisis ticks treated exactly: at a crisis tick T, with `X` the layer after it, (i) the moves without the crisis action are valued by the controllable predecessor on the analysis build in which the crisis action is unavailable (a guard part `dropped: FALSE`, as in docs/prc_experiment.md); (ii) the crisis branch is valued by the lifted value `L(s)`: for each set of exit levels e of the sub-game, `R_e` is the set of coarse states whose return transition with exit e lands in `X` (the controllable predecessor of `X` on a one-move build whose crisis action has e as its effect), and `L(s)` holds iff the coalition can force the sub-game (explored explicitly once) into terminal fine states whose exits e all have `s ∈ R_e`; (iii) the layer is `phi ∩ (Term ∪ CPre_dropped(X) ∪ (crisis available ∩ L))` when the crisis actor is in the coalition and `phi ∩ (Term ∪ (CPre_dropped(X) ∩ (crisis unavailable ∪ L)))` when it is not. This is analysis code over the public `wavefront.symbolic` functions (`cpre`, predicates) and compiled analysis builds, not a framework change; it is written with the test bodies and checked state by state against the composite engine from 1972 and later (where both run) before first sight. **Implemented (2026-10-24, before first sight)** in `tests/coldwar_v1/layered.py` (`Lift`; `lift_of`, `layered_kernel` and `layered_distance` in tests/coldwar_v1/common.py), with no change to `wavefront/`: the analysis build "crisis action unavailable" is written as a mask on the same encoding's availability (identical controllable predecessor, and the BDDs stay in one manager); `R_e(X)` is the preimage of `X` with the exit's levels substituted, restricted to the return move (valid because no exit variable is written by a delayed effect and the coarse model has no latch, both checked when a `Lift` is built); each sub-game is explored once per value of the coarse variables its entry reads (Able Archer: `belief_USSR_USA`); kernels and attractors are the backward layers of (iii); distances are weighted backward layers (a coarse move 1, a crisis with exit e 2 plus the fewest fine steps to a terminal with that exit, and, for the distance out of a kernel, a fine state outside the lifted kernel 1 plus its fine distance); strategy moves are read explicitly from the layers. Like the symbolic engine, transitions the explicit engine would reject (an exit lowering a monotone variable) are not excluded. The explicit form (`Window`, R V6) and the composite engine's distances (`dag_distance`: one pass over the acyclic flattened graph, the symbolic all-actor rank at the cut) are in the same module. **Validation** (tests/coldwar_v1/test_yearwise.py, run in the default suite, no claim's quantity computed): state by state against the explicit engine on the flattened game itself, for every coalition, kernel and attractor membership of every coarse state, lifted membership of every fine state, strategy moves, and the fewest transitions to a target and out of a kernel, on the hand-built game and 120 random games of tests/engine/test_multires.py, on a synthetic game with two crises at different ticks (an entry reading a coarse variable, multi-variable exits, a delayed action, and a restricted game), and on the toy standoff; the explicit form against the flattened game cut one tick after a one-occasion crisis on random games, and `dag_distance` against breadth-first search; on cw_v1, with predicates and targets no V claim reads (`esc = peace`, `¬us_began_war`, `esc ≥ crisis`, `esc = crisis`), every state of the flattened game from the 1983 hindsight state to the horizon (Able Archer inside), the explicit form against the flattened game cut at 1963 from the 1961 hindsight state (Cuba inside), and (marked slow) the composite engine from the 1975 hindsight state, every state of its explicit part; and the conditions above on the three builds under both hypotheses (exits read only the fine state, entries constant except Able Archer's, the crisis ticks 1948, 1962, 1969, 1973, 1983). During development a further 340 random games and the explicit form on 300 more agreed with the explicit engine. The bounded fallback below is therefore not used. The same scheme gives attractors (least fixpoint per layer) and distances (forward layers with the crisis as one more step). **If it cannot be written and checked before first sight**, the bounded fallback is: for the years outside the composite engine's budget, the coarse kernels with the sub-games inert, each so computed marked "coarse approximation" and the year counted as undecided, so that no claim passes on it (V2, V5's unbounded columns, V7 for 1964, V8).
- **Lifting** (v0's D5) from a sub-game's entry: the sub-game is explored explicitly and each terminal fine state is valued by its return state, read by the composite engine (exact) or, where the claim is bounded, by the bounded predicate at the return (exact).
- **Bounded** (the Z3 encoding unrolled from a state, as v0's R C12) on the coarse game, for relaxation counts. Exact where no sub-game can change the target in the window (stated per claim).
- A result on the coarse game with the sub-games inert is printed beside where the registration says so and decides no claim (section 9.2).
