# WW2 v1: architect's decisions for the model and the phase 2 tests (wave B)

Inputs: [ontology_ww2_v1.md](ontology_ww2_v1.md), [actions_ww2_v1.md](actions_ww2_v1.md), [ww2_v1_notes.md](ww2_v1_notes.md) (the historian's realized-sequence check, coding notes and test-by-test dependencies), the three v1 data files, [phase2_kickoff.md](phase2_kickoff.md) F1, F2, D1 and D2, and the v0 record ([ww2_v0_decisions.md](ww2_v0_decisions.md)). These decisions settle the open points the v1 content raised and fix, before any v1 test is run, the formal reading of every registered claim that v1 evaluates. Nothing here edits a registered claim; readings are logged as clarifications in ww2_testbed.md section 11. Where a claim cannot hold as read, the test fails, the report says why, and any amendment is proposed for the owner's decision, as in phase 1.

**Hold-out.** T6, T7, T11 and T12 are hold-out tests (kickoff D2). This document, like the v1 content, is written before any of them is run. After the first run of the v1 suite no guard, effect, delay, fairness constraint, profile or assume of `models/ww2/v1.py` changes without a row in [guard_changelog_ww2.md](guard_changelog_ww2.md) giving a source and a rerun of the whole suite; the report records the commit at which the hold-out tests were first run.

## E1. Start states, built-ins, tick convention

- "From state Q" in ww2_testbed.md section 5 is the state at the **start** of quarter Q, before that quarter's decisions: the hindsight file named for the previous quarter, at tick `index(Q)` (`index("1936Q1") == 0`), as the v0 tests read it. So T3 starts from `1938Q2.json` (tick 10), T9 from `1939Q1.json` (tick 13), T11 from `1940Q4.json` (tick 20), T6 from `1941Q1.json` (tick 21), T7 from `1941Q2.json` (tick 22), T12 from `1945Q1.json` (tick 37).
- Every v1 test loads the snapshot **with its built-ins** (`snapshot_state(..., builtins=True)`): the lesson of T8(b) in phase 1 is that a start state without its pending decisions measures the missing counters. The pending values are those of ww2_v1_notes.md section 3, item 8, computed by the builder from `data/ww2/decisions_ww2_v1.csv`.
- Tick convention, [SQR] and [NSL] as the notes state them (section 2). [NSL] (one Nature event per tick) is a limitation of a single Nature actor across two theatres, accepted and logged as a clarification; the report states it wherever a witness turns on the order of two Nature events.
- Fairness counters in the v1 snapshots are **computed, not assumed**: at the end of quarter Q, `fair_<name>` is the number of consecutive end-of-quarter states up to and including Q in which the trigger held and the actor's realized move of that quarter was not one of the responses, capped at `within` (the system update of framework_spec.md section 3.1 applied along the realized path). For `oil_clock` this gives 1 at the end of 1941Q3, 2 at the end of 1941Q4, 0 from the end of 1942Q1 (the oil is `secure`). Confidence follows the trigger's variables and the realized-move file.

Logged as a clarification of testbed section 4.6 for v1; v0 and its tests are unchanged.
9. `arms_GER = total` landing at 1941Q1 (record: 1942) and `arms_USSR = strong` at 1941Q3 (record: autumn 1942) are left as the historian wrote them: no v1 guard reads either level in the East, and the report lists both as known one-year discrepancies between the model and the snapshots.
10. The `talks_open` flag (T9) and the `atomic_use` fairness constraint (T12) are not adopted; `deterred` is adopted for T9 only (E8). The `prewar` part on Nature's `purge` **is** adopted: `purge` gets the part `prewar: not east_war`; the purge of the officer corps was a peacetime event of 1937 to 1938, and the wartime arrests of commanders (Pavlov, Jul 1941) did not destroy a command structure. ## E3. The oil clock and one well-formedness edit

A guard may read a fairness counter: `V.fair_oil_clock` compiles, explores and encodes (checked on a toy model with the explicit engine and the bounded checker before this document was written). `stocks_run_down:clock` reads `fair_oil_clock` as the catalogue writes it; no latch is needed.

One joint move is ill-formed as drafted: at peace with the counter at 8 (`within`), Nature must play `stocks_run_down` (`oil_JPN := critical`) and WEST may play `lift_embargo` (`oil_JPN := dependent`), two different `Set`s on one variable. Decision: `lift_embargo` gets the part `before_clock: fair_oil_clock < 8`. Reading: the quarter in which the stocks run out is Nature's; the embargo can be lifted the next quarter (`in_force` admits `critical`), at the cost of one quarter's delay in that one state. Logged in the guard changelog as a well-formedness edit made before any test, like v0's two. The model step checks every other pair of effects on one variable with the symbolic `reachable(check=True)` (or explicit exploration from the test start states) and logs any further edit the same way; two `Set`s of the **same** level in one joint move are not a conflict (compile.py `_compose`), so `attack_south` and `us_declare_war_jpn` at `pacific_war == colonial` need no edit.

## E4. The realized replay before any registered test

Before any registered v1 test is run, `data/ww2/realized_moves_v1.csv` is replayed from the tick-0 state under `aims_GER = hegemonic`, `aims_USSR = defensive` and each of `aims_JPN in {regional, hegemonic}` (the notes' hand check), with the delays of `decisions_ww2_v1.csv`: every row's actions must be available at its tick, the chain must end at tick 39 in `ALLIED_VICTORY`, and the replayed state at the end of each quarter is compared with the v1 hindsight snapshot, with the differences tabulated as in v0 (the one-tick [SQR] lags, the [NSL] placements, the two known one-year discrepancies of E2 item 9). This is T1's analogue for v1 and a well-formedness check, not a registered test; transcription errors it finds are fixed and logged as "coded before any registered test was run". Under `aims_JPN = status_quo` the realized row of 1941Q3 is unavailable, which the report records as the hypothesis's content.

## E5. Readings of the registered claims that v1 evaluates

The framework's notions are used throughout: ⟨⟨C⟩⟩ quantifies over every actor not in C, Nature included (framework_spec.md section 5.3); the contested region of `contested(kind='reach')` is "not forced by C and not blocked by the rest". Because Nature may decline every campaign resolution (`none`), no coalition of principals can force an outcome that needs a campaign won, so the standard notion makes "contested" trivially true wherever a campaign is involved. Every test that asserts "contested" therefore asserts the standard notion **and** reports the fortune variant: `attractor(C ∪ {Nature}, X)` ("C can reach X with the campaigns' favour whatever the other principals do") and `safe(rest principals ∪ {Nature}, ¬X)`, in a two-by-two table (each side's target forced with fortune: yes or no). A state is "strongly contested" when each side's target is reachable with fortune against the other's best play, which is the historian's sense of the word; the report names which sense each result is in.

### T6 (from 1941Q2, tick 21; `aims_GER = hegemonic`, `aims_USSR = defensive`, `aims_JPN` swept)

- "Control of western USSR plus the Caucasus": X := `w_ussr == german ∧ caucasus == german`. "Germany cannot force": ¬⟨⟨GER⟩⟩◇X (standard) with the fortune table; "contested for the 1941 campaign" per E5.
- V_GER (testbed 4.6, "states from which Germany can still force a non-defeat ending") := `safe({GER}, undefeated)` with the invariant `undefeated := reich == fighting ∧ ¬german_defeat`: a non-defeat ending is any ending other than `GERMAN_DEFEAT`, `ALLIED_VICTORY` and `PACIFIC_WAR_CONTINUES`, and all of those and only those have `reich == surrendered` or `german_defeat`, so keeping `undefeated` through the horizon is exactly reaching a non-defeat ending.
- "The quarter at which V_GER is left": the point of no return t_K of the framework along the realized path from tick 21 (the first realized state outside V_GER), reported with the loss point and the visible-failure tick, and with the driving variable from `sensitivity` at t_K (the variable whose one-level perturbation at the state before t_K restores membership, or changes the forced distance most). The window is 1941Q4 to 1943Q1 inclusive (ticks 23 to 28). Computed without a WEST profile first; `electoral` is a sensitivity run, since no profile is registered for T6.

### T7 (from 1941Q3, tick 22; `aims_JPN` swept; the hold-out test with the mining question)

- `war[JPN][USA]` := `pacific_war >= war`. "Embargo maintained" := the profile `embargo_maintained` (testbed changelog 2026-10-11). "The embargo": WEST's move at tick 22 is fixed to `embargo_jpn` in the bounded queries (the realized decision), so the embargo is visible from tick 23. "Within 8 ticks" := war visible at a tick ≤ 30 (eight ticks after the start state; the report also gives the tick at which Japan's option closes, so a one-tick ambiguity is visible). The GER-JPN pact := `anticomintern`.
- "Unavoidable unless the embargo is lifted or `aims_JPN = status_quo`": under the profile and `aims_JPN ∈ {regional, hegemonic}`, no play keeps `pacific_war == peace` through tick 30 (bounded checker: the target "peace at tick 30" is unreachable). If it is reachable as is, the witness is the finding and the first half fails as registered.
- "The minimal assumption set is exactly {embargo maintained, `aims_JPN ≠ status_quo`}": the minimal relaxation sets of the named assumptions (the profile, the hypothesis gates `JPN.<action>:hypothesis`, every guard part, the fairness `oil_clock`) that make "peace at tick 30" reachable, and their minimal hitting sets, which are the minimal sets of assumptions that by themselves keep war unavoidable. The claim holds when the only minimal hitting set is {`embargo_maintained`, the `hypothesis` gates of the attack actions}. Whatever the outcome, the report gives, per hypothesis and under the profile and without it: ⟨⟨JPN⟩⟩◇war at each tick from 22 to 30 (Japan's option), and the first tick at which `attack_south` is unavailable because `oil_JPN == critical` (the window the clock closes).
- "`strike_north` reachable only with the pact and western USSR collapsing": (a) `north_strike` reachable from tick 22 under `aims_JPN = hegemonic`; (b) unreachable when `w_ussr == german` is excluded at every tick (bounded query with the constraint), and unreachable from the start state with `anticomintern` counterfactually unset (a reported counterfactual initial state, since the pact holds from 1936Q4). The report states that (b) holds by construction of the guard written from the 1941 record, and that the hypothesis gate is a third condition the claim does not name. Any witness that uses `strike_colonies_only` or `seek_terms_east` is reported as resting on a speculative action.

### T9 (from 1939Q2, tick 13; `aims_GER = hegemonic`)

- {UK, FR, USSR} := {WEST, USSR}; `belief[GER][UK]` := `belief_GER`; "the attack on Poland" := GER `attack_poland`; "block" := keep `poland == independent` through tick 20 (the development window's end), as the invariant `poland_blocked := poland == independent ∨ tick > 20`, so that the kernel is computed to the v1 horizon without demanding anything after 1941Q1.

- `belief_GER` at the start state is swept over its three levels as counterfactual initial states (the snapshot reads `will_not_fight` at the end of 1939Q1; the model's `guarantee_poland` effect gives `uncertain` from tick 13). ### T11 (from 1941Q1, tick 20; `aims_JPN` swept; the profile `electoral`)

### T12 (from 1945Q2, tick 37; `aims_JPN` swept)

- "The atomic event ends the war" := Nature's `atomic` sets `pacific_war := surrendered`; "regardless of `aims_JPN`" := for each of the three levels, from the start state with its built-ins (`pending_WEST_atomic_program = 1`, so `bomb_ready` at tick 38), the trace in which Nature plays `atomic` at tick 38 exists and ends in `ALLIED_VICTORY`, and `atomic` reads no hidden objective (checked on the compiled guard). Nature may decline the event (actions catalogue); the report says so.
- "Only for a programme started at least 12 ticks earlier": the necessity direction. With `pending_WEST_atomic_program` swept over 0 to 12 in the start state (counterfactual start dates: pending p at tick 37 means the decision was taken at tick 24 + p, and `bomb_ready` is visible at tick 36 + p), the war ends by the bomb only for p ≤ 1, that is for a decision at tick ≤ 25, thirteen or more ticks before the event at 38. The claim's bound of 12 is implied (13 ≥ 12); the report gives the model's exact bound and the record's candidate start dates (notes section 6).

## E6. Engines

Kernels, attractors and contested regions on v1 use `wavefront.symbolic` where its tests pass and the computation fits in time (the benchmark of kickoff F1 is the guide); traces and relaxation sets use the bounded checker; the explicit engine only where the reachable set from the start state is small enough to explore whole. Where a kernel question does not fit, the fallback is the phase 1 sufficiency check (decisions D6): a fixed strategy and a bounded query, reported as "sufficient check by fixed strategy", never as the kernel. Every result names its engine.
