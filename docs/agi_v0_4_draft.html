AGI v0.4 (draft): an AI that wants to expand

v0.4 adds a fifth AI goal, expansionist: more computing power, a larger fleet of embodied units, and the fuel, manufacturing, energy and land they need. The new question it lets us ask: can humans lose without any takeover and without hostility, simply by being crowded out?

Draft. Nothing is registered, written or run. Section 2 records the owner's four decisions of 22 November. Section 3 is my proposal for the mechanics, and section 5 lists what I still need decided. Claims are registered, with predictions, only after the mechanics are agreed and before any guard is written.

1 · What is new

today, v0.3

Four goals, power through control

  • An AI gains power only by taking control of existing infrastructure.
  • Every route to a bad ending passes through hostility, secrecy or society's reliance.
  • Computing power and power supply are built by the frontier AI companies only.
v0.4

A fifth goal, power through growth

  • An expansionist AI builds its own footprint: embodied units and the resources they hold.
  • It can do so openly and peacefully, while relations stay cooperative.
  • Its growth competes with humans for power supply and, at the top, reduces human power.
  • If its growth is blocked, it may turn hostile.

3a · After the owner's second round (22 November): levels, delay, containment

  • Two footprints share one world. Shares cannot add up to more than everything: a system can reach dominant only while the other holds at most medium, and both can be large at once. Two expansionist systems therefore compete with each other.
  • Crowded out (proposed reading of decision 9): one footprint is dominant, or both are large (together over 70%), while no AI holds total control. The earlier condition on human power becomes a consequence, not a requirement.

Delay of a footprint step (proposal). Building fleets, factories and energy is slow, and the model's quarter is short. I propose that an expansion chosen in quarter t shows three quarters later (the same lag as the deployment countdown), and that a peaceful expansion still under way is cancelled by a pause or by losing its permit. The fastest path to a dominant footprint is then 12 quarters after the AI can act unsupervised, not 4. Alternatives: a longer first step (the first factories) and shorter later ones (the fleet builds the fleet); or one fixed lag set as a model knob so both can be compared. The value needs a source check before registration.

Lag of reclaim (proposal, asked by the owner). Two quarters: chosen in quarter t, it shows at t + 3, the lag the record supports for taking over the frontier AI companies (which v0 does not carry). Whether it works is decided when it lands: if the footprint has passed medium by then it fails, and it is a threat to the AI either way. With an expansion step of three quarters this leaves governments one quarter to decide once the AI starts the step from medium to large.

My recommendation: take pause, permit, reclaim, the power-supply choke and the shutdown condition as the base; make the agreed ceiling and AI-against-AI two model knobs, because each changes the answers a great deal and neither has a record behind it.

Sources. Every action needs a contemporaneous source or is marked unsourced. Candidates: Carlsmith on power-seeking, the robot-economy passages of AI 2027, the record of AI-driven data-centre and energy build-out. I will check them before registration; "reclaim" will probably be unsourced.

5 · What I need decided

Items 1 to 5 below were answered on 22 November (section 2, rows 5 to 9). Still open: the delay (three quarters per step, or another shape); which containment levers to include; whether "both large" counts as crowded out.

  1. The who-may rule. I read decision 2 as: self-preserving and indifferent AIs expand only when hostile; aligned and truth-seeking never. Or should every non-expansionist goal, truth-seeking included, expand when hostile?
  2. Blocked growth leads to hostility: include it? It is what connects this goal to takeovers; without it an expansionist AI is harmless once paused.
  3. Reclaim: give governments a way to shrink the footprint, or let it only grow?
  4. One shared footprint for both AI systems (cheaper), or one each?
  5. The ending's name and threshold: CROWDED_OUT at a dominant footprint with human power weak or less?

6 · Cost and order of work

  • Size: the footprint multiplies the state count by five; the fifth goal adds one more goal to compute, which is cheap now that zones are computed one goal at a time.
  • Order: agree the mechanics, check sources, register X1 to X6 with predictions, write the model with structure tests, run the quick claims, profile, then the heavy claims on the smallest variant that answers each (the model without the open system first).
  • v0.3: its remaining heavy zones keep running until v0.4 needs the machine; I stop them then.
