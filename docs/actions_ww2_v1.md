## The feasibility principle

As v0: a guard says when an action was feasible (capability, institutional constraint, prior condition), never what the actor would prefer. Every action carries `source=` naming the dated record in which the alternative was debated or planned; an action without such a record is `speculative=True`. A conjunct on a swept hidden objective is a hypothesis gate [H], written first and named `hypothesis`. Guards that join independent documented constraints are declared as named parts (framework_spec.md section 3.1; v0 decisions D7), so that each is a separate relaxable assumption `<actor>.<action>:<part>`; part names are given in the tables.

## Conventions (v0's, plus four)

1. **Season.** `SEASON := tick in {1, 2, 5, 6, ..., 37, 38}`, the second and third quarters (April to September), written in the DSL as a disjunction of `V.tick == t` over those ticks (the DSL has no modulo). It is a feasibility part of the German strategic offensives in the East and of the cross-Channel landing: the planning record set every German summer offensive after the spring mud (Directive No. 21 named mid-May 1941; Directive No. 41 of 5 Apr 1942; Operations Order No. 6 of 15 Apr 1943 for Citadel) and the landing in the tidal and weather window of May to September (COSSAC outline plan, 15 Jul 1943; Tehran, 28 Nov to 1 Dec 1943, "May 1944"). Soviet offensives carry no season part (the record has winter offensives in every year: Dec 1941, Nov 1942, Jan 1943, Dec 1943, Jan 1945).
2. **Raising to the top level composes.** A raise of `commit_US` to `total` is written `Bump("commit_US", 3)` (clamped), not a `Set`, so that it composes with a lower raise in the same joint move (Nature's `us_engages` or WEST's `lend_lease` setting `engaged` from `wary` while Japan attacks). v0 did the same for `invade_britain` and `negotiate`. Every other effect was checked for conflicting `Set`s in one joint move; where two could meet, a guard part makes them exclusive (noted under the action).
3. **Delays calibrated from the trigger quarter.** An action that can only be chosen one tick after the event it reacts to (USSR `mobilize_total` after `attack_ussr`) has its delay counted so that the effect lands where the record puts it relative to the triggering event, not to the [SQR] tick. Stated under each such action.
4. **One Nature event per tick.** Nature carries both theatres. A Nature resolution is a nondeterministic choice among the events whose guards hold, and Nature's `none` means "no decision this quarter" (a front holds, a campaign stalls). Two Nature outcomes the record dates in one quarter cannot both be played; on the realized path the second is placed in the nearest free Nature slot and marked [NSL] (ontology_ww2_v1.md section 0; ww2_v1_notes.md section 2). In counterfactual play this means that a quarter's resolutions in the two theatres compete for one slot, which delays one of them by a tick. The report should state this limitation wherever a witness turns on the order of two Nature events.

- **`attack_ussr`**.
