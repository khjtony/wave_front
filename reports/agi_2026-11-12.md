## 2. G2: the four conditions of the silent-takeover cluster (did not hold)

The claim: within the search length (24 quarters) the cluster needs all four conditions, and removing any one blocks it. Each condition was negated in turn.

|---|---|---|

Minimal sets that restore the ending under the energy cap, each of size 1: `Nature.breakthrough:compute` or `L.scale:energy`; under `indifferent` also `A.acquire_covert:cap`. None reads `regime`.

Why the conditions do not block (diagnostic paths, [agi_v0_witness_traces.md](agi_v0_witness_traces.md); each is one shortest path, not the cluster):

- **(1) and (2): oversight is not monotone.** `L.safety_invest` raises `oversight` to strong at position 2 or 4, and `L.open_weights` or `L.race` lowers it to weak before the first acquisition. Having been strong does not protect.
- **(3) society's reliance on AI: `reach` has a branch that does not read it.** `H.grant_autonomy` after `L.deploy_agents` takes `a_autonomy` to `self_directed`, and `reach` then holds whatever `dependence` is. At the quarter `acquired` is set, `dependence` is low (`self_preserving`) or med (`indifferent`).
- **(3) energy: the cap acts on the path to `cap = agi`, not on acquisition.** `L.scale` is the only action that raises `compute`, it costs one level of `energy_slack`, and `Nature.breakthrough` needs `compute ≥ c3`. From `energy_slack = med` one `scale` is affordable and enough. At the quarter `acquired` is set `energy_slack` is low on both paths, so "`energy_slack ≥ med`" at acquisition is not needed.
- **(4): continuity rights close `A.defect` only.** With `a_rights = continuity` held, no path uses `defect` (G4), and the human-AI relation turns hostile another way: `A.exfiltrate` (position 5), `Nature.detection` (6), then `H.shutdown_attempt` failing with `oversight = none` (7), which sets `trust = hostile`. Three steps of taking control, in secret or openly, follow.

The paths are identical in length with `unknown_capability` off, and none uses `A.seize`: the unsourced (assumed) route is not the reason.
