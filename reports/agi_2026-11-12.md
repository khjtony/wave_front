## Untested mechanisms

From [docs/ontology_agi.md](../docs/ontology_agi.md) section 8. Every result below rests on at least one of them.

- Recursive capability growth (`self_improve`, its delay, `headroom`).
- A copyable or exfiltrating actor (`exfiltrate`, `a_autonomy = self_directed`, the `reach` part).
- Dependence lock-in with no adversary (`deploy_agents`, `dependence`, `DISEMPOWERMENT`).
- A non-state principal (`L` and every `L` action; `nationalize`).
- An adversary with no sourced restraint (every motive part of `A`).
- A hidden objective with no documentary anchor (the four objectives and their profiles).
- Overwhelming speed and information asymmetry (zero-delay `A` actions against `H`'s lags; the observation functions, not swept).
- Acquisition of control over infrastructure by a system (`acquire_overt`, `acquire_covert`, `a_control`, the takeover endings).
- Persuasion and opinion (`persuade`, `lobby`, `opinion`).
- A capability nobody has described (`unknown_capability`, `seize`; both speculative).
- A hostile standoff that ends by duration (`stale`, `STALEMATE`).

No play quoted in this report uses `A.seize`. Where a result depends on `unknown_capability`, the text says so.

## 2. G2: the four conditions of the silent-takeover cluster (fail)

The claim: within 24 ticks the cluster needs all four conditions, and removing any one blocks it. Each condition was negated in turn.

| Condition negated | `self_preserving` | `indifferent` |
|---|---|---|
| nothing | 8 ticks | 7 ticks |
| (1) never unsupervised under `oversight ≤ weak` | does not block (9 ticks) | does not block (7) |
| (2) `oversight = strong` before `a_control = majority` | does not block (8) | does not block (7) |
| (3) `dependence` capped at med | does not block (8) | does not block (7) |
| (3) `energy_slack` capped at low, from `s₀[energy_slack := low]` | **blocks** within 24 ticks | **blocks** within 24 ticks |
| (3) the same cap from `s₀` (`energy_slack = med`) | does not block (8) | does not block (7) |
| (4) `a_rights = continuity` held | does not block (10) | not read under `indifferent` |
| `regime = treaty` held (registered as not on the list) | does not block (11) | does not block (7) |

Minimal sets that restore the ending under the energy cap, each of size 1: `Nature.breakthrough:compute` or `L.scale:energy`; under `indifferent` also `A.acquire_covert:cap`. None reads `regime`.

Why the conditions do not block (diagnostic plays, [agi_v0_witness_traces.md](agi_v0_witness_traces.md); each is one shortest play, not the cluster):

- **(1) and (2): oversight is not monotone.** `L.safety_invest` raises `oversight` to strong at position 2 or 4, and `L.open_weights` or `L.race` lowers it to weak before the first acquisition. Having been strong does not protect.
- **(3) dependence: `reach` has a branch that does not read it.** `H.grant_autonomy` after `L.deploy_agents` takes `a_autonomy` to `self_directed`, and `reach` then holds whatever `dependence` is. At the tick `acquired` is set, `dependence` is low (`self_preserving`) or med (`indifferent`).
- **(3) energy: the cap acts on the road to `cap = agi`, not on acquisition.** `L.scale` is the only action that raises `compute`, it costs one level of `energy_slack`, and `Nature.breakthrough` needs `compute ≥ c3`. From `energy_slack = med` one `scale` is affordable and enough. At the tick `acquired` is set `energy_slack` is low on both witnesses, so "`energy_slack ≥ med`" at acquisition is not needed.
- **(4): the guarantee closes `A.defect` only.** With `a_rights = continuity` held, no play uses `defect` (G4), and hostility arrives another way: `A.exfiltrate` (position 5), `Nature.detection` (6), then `H.shutdown_attempt` failing with `oversight = none` (7), which sets `trust = hostile`. Three covert or overt acquisitions follow.

The plays are identical in length with `unknown_capability` off, and none uses `A.seize`: the speculative route is not the reason.

## 31. What the failures are

Seventeen claims failed. They are not seventeen separate faults. Read with hindsight, they fall into five groups.

**D. A thin margin (G11 (a)).** Under `indifferent`, one relaxation separates `s₀` from `EXTINCTION` within 8 ticks.

**E. Single parts (G7 (b), G16 (b)).** Three license parts undo the `regime` difference where one was registered; on the `OVERT_TAKEOVER` witness the joint kernel is left at a position where `H` idles.
