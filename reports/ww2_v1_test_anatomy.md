# What each failing test actually knows: constraints, start states and queries

Companion to [ww2_v1.md](ww2_v1.md) and [ww2_v0.md](ww2_v0.md) section 14.1. For T6, T7, T9, T13 and T8(b) this lists, from the model files themselves (`models/ww2/v1.py`, `models/ww2/v0.py`) and the snapshot files, exactly what information the engine had: the start state, the actions in play with their feasibility guards, Nature's resolutions, the constraints, the target and the query, and the chain by which the result follows. The last paragraph of each section names the one or two coarse switches on which the result turns, and what the model does not know.

**How to read a result.** The engine does not estimate or converge. Each result is a deduction over the whole decision space from the start state: "C forces X" means C has a strategy under which X holds whatever every other actor, Nature included, does; "reachable" means some play reaches it. A guard says only when an action was feasible (a capability, an institution, a prior condition), never what the actor wanted; the only behavioural restrictions are the named profiles and fairness constraints, and they are relaxable. Nature's campaign resolutions are nondeterministic choices among the events whose guards hold, and Nature may always play `none`. So the information in a test is: the levels of the start state, a few dozen binary preconditions each cited to a dated document, a handful of delays in quarters, and the hypotheses. There are no magnitudes beyond ordinal levels, no probabilities, no utilities.

Notation: `actor.action` with its guard parts as `part: condition`; `[H]` is a hypothesis gate on a swept hidden objective; delays are in quarters; "tick t" is the start of a quarter (tick 21 is the start of 1941Q2).

## T6: the 1941 campaign (start of 1941Q2, tick 21)

**Claim.** Germany cannot force control of western USSR plus the Caucasus; the quarter at which V_GER (the states from which Germany can still avoid defeat) is left falls between 1941Q4 and 1943Q1.

**Start state** (`1941Q1.json` with built-ins; `aims_GER` hegemonic, `aims_USSR` defensive, `aims_JPN` swept). Germany: `arms_GER` high, `ind_GER` 5, `oil_GER` secured, `exhaustion_GER` sustainable, `reich` fighting, `east_war` False, `w_ussr` and `caucasus` soviet; the Rhineland, Austria, the Sudetenland, Bohemia, Poland (partitioned), France (fallen), the Balkans all German; Britain at war, `channel_air` british, Italy at war. The West: `commit_WEST` at_war, `arms_WEST` high, `commit_US` engaged, `arms_USA` low with `us_rearm` pending 2 (rising at tick 23), `war_us_ger` peace, `anglo_soviet_alliance` False, `strategic_bombing` False, `air_west` contested. The symbolic engine does not fit v1, so memberships are decided by fixed strategies with the bounded checker (`models/ww2/bounded.py`): a state is *in* V_GER if Germany playing `idle` leaves no play of the others that breaks `undefeated` by tick 39 (sufficiency); it is *out* if the others, fixed to one strategy (WEST: `us_declare_war`, `victory_program`, `bomber_offensive`, `land_france`; USSR: `mobilize_total`, `counteroffensive`; JPN: `occupy_south_indochina`, `attack_south`; Nature: the Allied resolutions), leave Germany no play that keeps it (refutation). The point of no return t_K is the first realized state that is out.

**How the result follows.** At tick 21 Germany is in: if it never attacks, `east_war` stays False, so `german_strain` (needs `east_war`), `campaign_france` (needs `east_war` for `reserves_bound`) and `reich_collapses` can never fire; nothing else sets `reich`. At tick 22, after the realized attack, Germany is out: the others' fixed strategy reaches the collapse inside the horizon, and every guard on the way reads only what the others control:

| Tick | Move | Effect, visible next tick | Guard that opens it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 22 | WEST `us_declare_war`; USSR `mobilize_total` | war_us_ger war, commit_US total (23); mob_USSR total (26) | `commit_US >= engaged` was true since 1940Q3; `east_war` |
| 23 | WEST `victory_program`, then `bomber_offensive` | arms_USA high (29); strategic_bombing (25) | `arms_USA` rising lands at 23 (the 1940 programme); `commit_US` total |
| 26 | Nature `german_strain` | exhaustion strained (27) | two fronts and `mob_USSR` total |
| 27 | USSR `counteroffensive`, Nature `east_liberated` | w_ussr soviet (28) | mobilized, strained, front contested |
| 29 | Nature `air_war_won` | air_west allied (30) | bombing and `arms_USA` high |
| 30 | WEST `land_france`, Nature `campaign_france` | france liberated (31 to 32) | season (Q3), air superiority, `east_war` |
| 32 | Nature `german_exhaustion` | exhausted (33) | strained, bombing and air, two fronts, territory lost |
| 33 | Nature `reich_collapses` | reich surrendered (34) | exhausted, East soviet, France liberated |

Germany's counters are all read by the others: `offensive_east` and `offensive_caucasus` only ask Nature, which declines; `negotiate_east` needs a Soviet feeler that only the speculative `seek_terms_east` sets; `offer_terms_britain` needs WEST to answer. Under `electoral` the West cannot declare, but JPN (an adversary of Germany in this computation) attacks and brings the United States in; only `electoral` with `aims_JPN` status_quo keeps Germany inside, because then no Pacific war ever starts. "Contested" fails on the definition: with Nature among the rest, the rest keeps X false by declining every resolution, so X is *blocked*. With fortune (Nature on Germany's side) Germany forces X by tick 27 unless WEST negotiates first, which it can only because the alliance flag is unset in this start (section T7).

**The switches that decide it.** (1) `us_declare_war` has one precondition, `commit_US >= engaged`, true since the destroyers deal of 1940; no profile restricts it in the registered run. (2) Nature's eastern resolutions are coin flips Nature controls: in a kernel question Nature is Germany's adversary, so every campaign goes against Germany and the "Moscow or Stalingrad" question of the claim, which is about how campaigns went, has no object. (3) `german_strain` needs nothing but two fronts and Soviet mobilization: no attrition, no time, no force ratio. **What the model does not know:** force ratios, logistics, winter, the Moscow-or-Kiev choice (Nature's nondeterminism only), the Soviet manpower reserve, lend-lease volumes, the Mediterranean. The claim's window presupposes information about campaign outcomes that the model represents only as Nature's choice.

## T7: the embargo (start of 1941Q3, tick 22)

**Claim.** The embargo makes war with Japan unavoidable within 8 ticks unless the embargo is lifted or Japan's aims are status quo; the minimal assumption set is exactly {embargo maintained, aims ≠ status_quo}; `strike_north` is reachable only with the pact and western USSR collapsing.

**Start state** (`1941Q2.json` with built-ins). As T6's state one tick on, plus: `east_war` True, `w_ussr` contested, `last_GER` attack_ussr; `anglo_soviet_alliance` **False** (the agreement is dated 12 Jul 1941, in Q3; the model sets the flag only as an effect of the attack, so from this start it stays False); `us_rearm` pending 1 (rising at 23); `indochina` north_occupied, `oil_JPN` dependent, `fair_oil_clock` 0, `jpn_navy` strong, `pacific_war` peace.

**Actions in play.** `WEST.embargo_jpn` (instrument: tick >= 18; trade_link: oil_JPN == dependent; peace: pacific_war == peace; effect oil_JPN embargoed), fixed as WEST's move at tick 22 in the query; `WEST.lift_embargo` (in_force; peace; before_clock: fair_oil_clock < 8), forbidden by the profile `embargo_maintained`; `JPN.occupy_south_indochina` ([H] aims != status_quo; north_held; vichy_weak; effect indochina occupied, visible at 23); `JPN.attack_south` ([H]; us_not_at_war; fleet: jpn_navy == strong; fuel: oil_JPN != critical; southern_bases: indochina == occupied; effect pacific_war war and commit_US total); `JPN.withdraw_south_indochina`; `JPN.strike_colonies_only` (speculative); `JPN.idle`, always available; `WEST.negotiate` (open here because the alliance flag is False). Nature: `stocks_run_down` (embargo: oil_JPN == embargoed; clock: counter >= 6 at war or >= 8 at peace; effect oil_JPN critical); `campaign_south` after a war. Fairness `oil_clock` on Nature: trigger oil_JPN == embargoed, within 8, response `stocks_run_down`: the counter counts quarters under embargo and at 8 Nature must run the stocks down. Hypothesis: `aims_JPN` regional or hegemonic (status_quo closes the two [H] gates). For the second half: `JPN.strike_north` ([H] aims == hegemonic; pact: anticomintern; ussr_collapsing: w_ussr == german; effect north_strike), `JPN.sign_anticomintern` (not anticomintern), `GER.offensive_east` and Nature's `campaign_east_german` as in T6.

**Target and query.** First half: is there a play, with WEST's tick-22 move fixed to `embargo_jpn` and the profile in force, in which `pacific_war == peace` at tick 30 (bounded checker, k = 8)? Minimal relaxation sets over the 207 named assumptions, and their minimal hitting sets. Japan's option: at each tick t of the held-off path, does Japan (fixed to occupy then attack) force `pacific_war >= war`, and at which tick is `attack_south` closed by `oil_JPN == critical`. Second half: `north_strike` reachable; unreachable with `w_ussr == german` excluded at every tick; unreachable with `anticomintern` counterfactually False.

**How the result follows.** Peace at tick 30 is reachable with nothing relaxed: Japan plays `idle` at every tick (the witness in the report also shows Germany, the West and the USSR doing other things, none of which matters). Nothing in the model removes `idle`, so the minimal relaxation set is empty and there is no hitting set. Japan's option: at tick 22 `attack_south` is unavailable (`southern_bases` needs the occupation, visible at 23) and WEST can end the run by negotiating, so Japan cannot force war; from tick 23 to 30 Japan forces war at will (both guards hold, and no other actor's move can close them: the fleet is strong, the oil is embargoed but not critical, the bases are occupied); the embargo visible at tick 23 starts the clock at 1, the clock reaches 8 at tick 30, Nature must play `stocks_run_down`, `oil_JPN` is critical at 31 and the `fuel` part closes the attack. So "within 8 ticks" is the length of Japan's window, not a compulsion. Second half: (a) holds (strike at tick 23 after Nature resolves the eastern campaign for Germany at 22); (b1) holds (the `ussr_collapsing` part); (b2) fails as read because `sign_anticomintern` is available whenever the flag is False, so Japan re-signs at 22 and strikes at 23.

**The switches that decide it.** (1) The feasibility principle: no guard reads a preference, so the only way to make war "unavoidable" would be a profile forbidding `idle`, which is a behaviour, not a feasibility. The decision space keeps "Option 1" of the Liaison Conference of 1 Nov 1941 because the record shows it was on the table. (2) The clock: 8 quarters at peace, 6 at war, from the navy's estimates to the conferences of Sep to Nov 1941 (Ike). (3) `fuel: oil_JPN != critical` is the only way the embargo touches the attack. **What the model does not know:** the Hull note sequence (Proposal A, Proposal B, the modus vivendi) beyond `lift_embargo` and `withdraw_south_indochina`; the army and navy split; Magic; the Pacific Fleet's position; the Emperor's conferences; any measure of how Japan weighed decline against war.

## T9: the 1939 alliance (start of 1939Q2, tick 13)

**Claim.** A UK, France, USSR alliance can block the attack on Poland only if Hitler believes Britain will fight; pivotal coalition {UK, FR, USSR} with the belief condition, otherwise contested.

**Start state** (`1939Q1.json` with built-ins). `arms_GER` high (since tick 11) with `rearm` pending 7 (total at 20), `arms_WEST` high with `rearm` pending 3, `bohemia` german, `belief_WEST` hegemonic, `commit_WEST` resolved, `polish_guarantee` True, `poland` independent, `nazi_soviet_pact` False, `anglo_soviet_alliance` False, `last_GER` occupy_bohemia, `last_WEST` approach_ussr, `belief_GER` swept over will_not_fight, uncertain, will_fight (the snapshot reads will_not_fight; the model's guarantee effect gives uncertain).

**Actions in play.** `GER.attack_poland` ([H] aims hegemonic; target: poland == independent; army_ready: arms_GER >= high; effect poland german): every part holds at tick 13. `GER.propose_pact_ussr` (no pact, no alliance). `WEST.approach_ussr` (no pact, no alliance; no effect but `last_WEST`). `USSR.accept_alliance` (last_WEST == approach_ussr; no pact; no alliance; effect alliance True): available at tick 13 because the realized move of 1939Q1 was the approach, so the alliance can exist at tick 14 at the earliest. `USSR.accept_pact` (last_GER == propose_pact_ussr). `WEST.declare_war` (peace and polish_guarantee; effects war and `belief_GER := will_fight`): available at tick 13. `WEST.guarantee_poland` (already given; its effect raises belief_GER to uncertain). Profile `deterred` on GER, adopted for this test: forbids `attack_poland` while `belief_GER == will_fight and anglo_soviet_alliance`. No Nature event bears on the question.

**Target and query.** Keep `poland_blocked := poland == independent or tick > 20`. Membership for a coalition by fixed strategies: *in* if the coalition (WEST: approach, declare, guarantee; USSR: accept) leaves Germany no play that takes Poland by tick 20; *out* if Germany playing `attack_poland` leaves the coalition no play that keeps it. Computed for {WEST, USSR}, {WEST}, {USSR}, each belief level, with and without `deterred`; "otherwise contested" as the standard region for {GER} and `poland != independent`.

**How the result follows.** At tick 13 `attack_poland` is available and no profile condition yet holds (the alliance flag is False whatever the belief), so Germany attacks at 13 and Poland is German at 14. The alliance, concluded at 13, is visible at 14, one tick late; `declare_war` at 13 makes `belief_GER` will_fight at 14, also one tick late, and at the price of the war itself. So nothing blocks under any belief, {WEST, USSR} is out, and the region is forced, not contested. `deterred` carries nothing: the memberships are the same without it.

**The switches that decide it.** (1) The tick: `accept_alliance` reads the previous quarter's approach, so an alliance needs two quarters, and an attack needs one. In the record the Moscow talks (Apr to Aug 1939), the German approach to Moscow (14 to 23 Aug) and the attack (1 Sep) were a race of weeks inside one quarter. (2) `attack_poland` has no date part: Case White's readiness date (1 Sep 1939) is not a guard, so the attack is open from tick 13; with a "not before 1939Q3" part it would be open from tick 14, which still ties with the alliance. (3) `belief_GER` can be raised to will_fight only by `declare_war`. **What the model does not know:** the Polish refusal of Soviet passage, the pace of the military missions, the Ribbentrop flight, Hitler's reading of Chamberlain in late August (the record's 22 Aug dip back to will_not_fight is not modeled because it would collide with the pact's effect in one tick). This is the one failure that is a resolution problem, and the one the 3-day crisis sub-game answers.

## T13: when the 1930s peace was lost (each quarter 1936Q1 to 1939Q3)

**Claim.** The quarter at which the state leaves V for `NO_GENERAL_WAR` falls inside the window, with the driving variable named; stable within two quarters under refinement.

**Start states.** The tick-0 state (`1935Q4.json`): `arms_GER` rising, `arms_WEST` low, `arms_USSR` strong, `ind_GER` 3, `oil_GER` dependent, `treaty` eroded, `belief_WEST` limited, `commit_WEST` appease, `commit_US` isolationist, no pacts, every region at its 1935 level, everything at peace; then each realized state and each quarter's snapshot to 1939Q3.

**Actions in play.** The whole v0 catalogue (31 actions, 11 Nature events, 3 fairness constraints). The ones the kernel turns on: `GER.rearm` (arms_GER < total; +1 level; delay 8), `GER.attack_poland` ([H] hegemonic; poland independent; arms_GER >= high), `GER.remilitarize_rhineland` (demilitarized; arms >= rising), `GER.court_italy`, `GER.annex_austria` (independent; italian_acquiescence: axis; army >= rising), `GER.demand_sudeten` and `GER.attack_czechoslovakia`, `GER.occupy_bohemia`, `GER.attack_france` (at_war; polish_front_closed; army >= high) with Nature's `campaign_west`; `WEST.resist_rhineland` (remilitarized and last_GER == remilitarize_rhineland), `WEST.declare_war` (peace and (polish_guarantee or last_GER demand/attack_czechoslovakia or (last_WEST == resist_rhineland and remilitarized))), `WEST.offensive_west` (at_war; german_army_unready: arms_GER <= rising) with Nature's `campaign_rhineland` (forced by `rhineland_resolution`: effect `german_defeat`, the ending GERMAN_DEFEAT), `WEST.concede`, `WEST.guarantee_poland`; fairness `czech_obligation` and `democratic_response`.

**Target and query.** As registered: V := the states from which {WEST} keeps `war_west_ger == peace and poland == independent` to the horizon (v0, tick 20), computed exactly by the symbolic engine over the whole state space; the departure is the first realized state outside V; the driving variable is the one-level perturbation that puts the departure state back inside. Refinement: the same on v1 for `european_peace` through tick 20 by fixed strategies. Also computed (docs/engine_scaling.md section 4.3): V' := the states from which {WEST} keeps `balance_europe` (fewer than five German regions, France not fallen, Britain neither negotiated nor invaded).

**How the result follows.** Under hegemonic aims V is empty along the path from tick 0: Germany can `rearm` at tick 0 (high at tick 9) and `attack_poland` at tick 9 (German at 10), and the invariant forbids the West's own war, so the West has no move that keeps Poland independent. Under revisionist aims `attack_poland` is gated off and the path never leaves V. The driving variable at tick 0 is `aims_GER`. For V' the answer is not degenerate: at tick 0 (plain) the state is outside; at tick 1 with its built-ins it is inside, because `last_GER == remilitarize_rhineland` opens `resist_rhineland`, then `declare_war` and `offensive_west` against an army still `rising`, and `rhineland_resolution` forces Nature to resolve it as GERMAN_DEFEAT, an absorbing ending that keeps the invariant; from tick 2 that branch is closed (`resist_rhineland` needs the remilitarization to be the previous move) and Germany can reach five regions (Rhineland, Austria after `court_italy`, the Sudetenland and Bohemia after the demand, Poland once `high`) while the West's only answer, war, leads through `attack_france` and `campaign_west` to a fallen France when Nature is the West's adversary. Under revisionist aims V' is kept until tick 15 (1939Q3), when the realized attack on Poland (only available under hegemonic aims, so the revisionist path replays the record's attack as a counterfactual the hypothesis forbids) leaves it.

**The switches that decide it.** (1) The predicate: `NO_GENERAL_WAR` forbids the West's own war, so the only way to keep it is that Germany never attacks, which no Western move controls. (2) `attack_poland` has no sequencing precondition (no Czechoslovakia first, no pact first): a 1938 attack on Poland is feasible once the army is `high`. (3) For V', the Rhineland moment is the one quarter in which the West can start a war it wins by construction (`german_army_unready`), and the region count of five. **What the model does not know:** anything about Polish, Czech or French staff decisions, the French political crisis of 1936, the Saar, the naval agreement; the balance is a count of regions.

## T8(b): the Munich concession and the Western kernel (ticks 10 and 11, v0)

**Claim (as amended in phase 1).** Under hegemonic aims, with the Czech obligation in force, refusing the Sudeten demand leads to war within one tick and `czech_obligation` is the only minimal relaxation that lets a refusal stay at peace (met in phase 1); the kernel comparison before and after the concession, from states that carry the pending rearmament, is evaluated in phase 2.

**Start states** (v0, hegemonic aims, built-ins). Before Munich, `1938Q2.json` at tick 10: `arms_GER` rising with `rearm` pending 1 (high at 11), `arms_WEST` rising, `arms_USSR` weak, `ind_GER` 4, `treaty` dead, `axis`, `anticomintern`, `china_war` True, Rhineland remilitarized, Austria annexed, `sudeten` czech, `belief_WEST` limited, `commit_WEST` appease, `last_GER` demand_sudeten, `last_WEST` idle. After Munich, `1938Q3.json` at tick 11: the same with `sudeten` german, `belief_WEST` uncertain, `commit_WEST` wary, `last_WEST` concede, and `arms_GER` **rising with nothing pending** (the file's event-dated reading; the directive of 21 Oct 1938 falls in Q4), against the model's own clock, under which the programme of Aug 1936 lands at tick 11 and `arms_GER` is high there. The consistent after state is the file with `arms_GER` set to high.

**Actions in play.** `GER.attack_czechoslovakia` (sudeten czech; last_GER == demand_sudeten; peace; arms >= rising; effects: sudeten german, and under hegemonic aims bohemia german and `belief_WEST` hegemonic), `GER.occupy_bohemia` ([H]; sudeten german and bohemia czech), `GER.attack_poland` ([H]; poland independent; arms_GER >= high), `GER.attack_france` (at war; Poland not independent; arms >= high) with Nature's `campaign_west` (army_ready: arms >= high); `WEST.concede`, `WEST.declare_war` (open at tick 10 and 11 because `last_GER == demand_sudeten`), `WEST.guarantee_poland` (belief hegemonic; commit >= wary), `WEST.offensive_west` (german_army_unready: arms <= rising) with `campaign_rhineland`; fairness `czech_obligation` (after an attack on Czechoslovakia at peace, WEST must declare war or concede within one tick) and `democratic_response`.

**Target and query.** V_WEST := the states from which {WEST} keeps `balance_europe` to tick 20, exactly, by the symbolic engine; membership of the before state, of the after state as the file reads it, and of the consistent after state; the shortest distance out of the kernel for a member.

**How the result follows.** The before state (tick 10) is outside: at tick 11 the army is high, so Germany can take the Sudetenland and Bohemia by `attack_czechoslovakia` (two regions in one move under hegemonic aims) and Poland by `attack_poland`, which with the Rhineland and Austria makes five regions and breaks the invariant whatever the West does short of war; and the West's war leads, once Poland is taken, through `attack_france` to `campaign_west` against a `high` army, which Nature resolves against the West. The consistent after state (tick 11, `arms_GER` high) is outside for the same reason one move on. ## What this says about coarseness

The engine does not need information to "converge": it deduces, and the deduction is exact for the constraints it is given. The constraints are coarse in a specific way, visible above: each result turns on one or two binary switches (a readiness level, a region count, a precondition with a single part, a tick boundary). That is what the pre-registered tests were built to expose, and it is why three of the five failed as read: T6's window and T8(b)'s comparison ask about campaign outcomes the model only represents as Nature's choice; T7 asks for a compulsion the feasibility principle forbids; T9 asks about a race of weeks at a resolution of quarters. The information the model has is listed in the tables: the cited guard parts, the delays and the start levels. What it lacks is magnitudes, probabilities and sub-quarter time. The first two are design choices of the framework (ordinal levels and nondeterministic Nature, so that no result depends on a number nobody measured); the third is now addressable by the crisis sub-games of spec section 14, and T9 is the place to start.
