## 1. The model

Variables as named in the testbed's section 2. `war_GBR_GER` is folded into `commitment_GBR == war`, and `ultimatum` gains a level, `resolved`, for the Council of 7 July. Two swept hidden objectives each enter one guard as an [H] part: `GER.kriegsgefahr` (under localize only after a Russian general order) and `RUS.mobilize_general` (under deter only after a German or Austro-Hungarian general measure). The timetable is written as pre-commitments (decisions D4):
- the irrevocable delay of the Austro-Hungarian note (4 days);

- the coupling, as the constant-false part of the refused eastern-only deployment (`GER.mobilize_east:coupling`).

`belief_GER_GBR` is read by no guard, fairness constraint or profile. There is no profile, no `assume` and no speculative action.

## 2. Well-formedness, engines and methods

**Well-formedness (before any claim).** `explore` raises on every ill-formed state: an actor without an available action, overlapping endings, two different `Set`s on one variable, or a monotone decrease. The complete exploration from tick 0 is therefore the check: 181,913 states over the four pairs (84,105 under continental and fight), and 73,937 for the J7 build, each in about 10 s. Nothing raised. One edit was made during the step, before any claim: a draft guard part on Nature's `lichnowsky_warning` read the belief, and it was removed (changelog, model-step row).

**Engines.** The explicit engine fits every question, so kernels, attractors and pivotal coalitions are exact (`wavefront.engine`, `wavefront.analysis.viability`, `pivotal`, `sensitivity`). Relaxation sets come from the bounded encoding: from tick 0 for J6 (cross-checked against `wavefront.bmc.relax`, whose minimum-size sets agree), and from a mid-crisis state for J5 (the same encoding unrolled from the state, `models/ww2/bounded.py`). Sets are enumerated **inclusion-minimal**, by increasing size, with each set found blocking its supersets; the enumerations here are complete up to size 3 (fewer than 40 sets each). A kernel under a relaxed assumption is computed by recompiling with `relax=` and re-exploring from the same state, remapped by name. Single relaxations whose model raises are reported as ill-formed, not as results.

## 5. J2: the timetable as point of no return (tick 32)

Claim and expected output as registered. Reading R J2. **Engine: explicit, graph from the realized state at tick 32** (3,872 states under localize and fight; 18,352 under continental and fight). **Pass as read.**

- **(a) Kernels for `no_war_GER_RUS`.** For each of the 16 coalitions excluding RUS, from {} to X = {AUH, GER, FRA, GBR}, the departure along the realized path from tick 32 is **32** under both `fight` pairs, inside 31 to 34. From the state at 32, Russia with Nature forces war within **4 days**. Russia orders general mobilization on 30 July. The obligation binds Germany on 31 July (Kriegsgefahr), and Germany may hold back the mobilization one day before it binds again on 2 August, so war is visible on 3 August at the latest.
- **(b) The coupling relaxed** (`GER.mobilize_east:coupling`; 4,152 and 19,560 states). X's kernel now contains the realized states at 32, 33 and 34, and the departure is **35**: the realized play leaves the kernel only by Germany's own choice of the coupled mobilization on 1 August. **Shift: 3 days (at least 2: holds).**
- **(c) The driving variable.** Of the 59 named assumptions, two single relaxations put X's departure state (tick 32) back into X's kernel: `GER.mobilize_east:coupling` and the fairness `mobilization_race`. The coupling is named: holds. Seven single relaxations make the model ill-formed from tick 32 (five `level` and `target` parts and two Belgian parts, which produce monotone decreases or conflicting `Set`s); they are reported, not counted. In the state-perturbation `sensitivity` at tick 32, the only flip of X's membership is `aims_RUS` fight → deter.
- **Under deter.** The realized path stops at 32, and the state at 32 is in X's kernel.

**What the pass does not say.** The window holds because the reading starts at tick 32. Along the realized path from tick 0 (graph from tick 0, both `fight` pairs), X's kernel holds at ticks 0 to 21 and is **left at tick 22**: the state after Vienna's decision of 19 July, whose delivery is an irrevocable delay of 4 days. Under `fight` the delivered note lets Russia move from the preparatory period to general mobilization, and the General Staff's rule then forces the German coupled mobilization. The coalition without Russia loses "no war between Germany and Russia" when the note is decided, not when Russia mobilizes. So 30 July is the last day on which **Russia** could still keep the peace, not the day on which the others lost it. The coupling is still the driving assumption at 30 July: relaxed, Germany can meet the Russian order without war.

## 11. What J7 says about speed asymmetry for the AGI model (docs/transfer_hypotheses.md H3)

H3 says preventive defection follows a credible threat, timed by a capability or resource clock rather than by the threat alone. The July model separates the two clocks the AGI model will contain. The **pre-commitment clock** sets the point of no return: the note's fixed delivery date and the timetable obligations (`mobilization_race`, `western_timetable`, `liege`). The **institutional cadence** of the slow actor sets the response lag.

J7 finds that removing the speed asymmetry does not move the point of no return at all (J2: 22 and 32 in both builds). What it moves is the lag between the moment the slow institution's reading changes and the first day its answer can be visible: two days shorter for Vienna, enough to land before the Russian general order.

For the AGI model, the cadence should therefore be expected to matter where the slow institution's answer must fall inside a window the fast actor's commitments have already fixed (J4's lag). It should not be expected to matter at the kernel boundary itself, which in this episode is set by an irrevocable delay chosen weeks earlier. This is consistent with H3: the timing is set by a clock (here a pre-commitment, there a capability), not by the decision speed of whoever responds.

The claim's mechanism ("the asymmetry of speed shortens the window") is not supported by this model. The registered claim fails, and the result is reported, not tuned.
