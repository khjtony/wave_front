Open weights: what changes in what can be guaranteed

v0.2 adds four consequences of an open-weight release to the model. The kernel claims ask what each one does to the guarantees governments and labs hold. Retraining by the public is the one that removes a guarantee. Race pressure, against the prediction, does not. The overall balance (E5) could not be computed on the present machine.

Everything here describes what the model's rules allow. It is not a forecast. A "guarantee" is something a group can keep true whatever the others and chance do.

A · No recall

Works as written; no guarantee tested

A pause no longer stops a pending adoption wave once weights are open. A treaty still does. The slide into disempowerment is still 13 quarters.

B · Race pressure

Does not cost the control guarantee

Governments and labs together still guarantee control from today under every objective, as without it. The prediction was that they would lose it.

C · Outside testing

Does not rescue governments alone

Governments alone still cannot guarantee control. On the fastest takeover roads the last move is no longer the labs' alone.

D · Retraining by the public

Removes the benefit of starting aligned

From an aligned start a silent takeover is possible in 7 quarters, and governments alone lose the coexistence guarantee under every starting objective.

E3 (b) · Why race pressure does not break the guarantee

The pressure says: once weights are open, the labs must deploy or race at least every third quarter, whenever one of the two is available to them. The prediction was that this takes away the labs' option to hold back, and with it the joint guarantee of control.

The result is that governments and labs together still hold it, under every starting objective. The doubt recorded with the prediction looks right: governments can take over the labs in the first quarter, which blocks both deploying and racing, so the pressure never binds. The guarantee survives, but only through a move governments score as exceptional. The model computed the membership; that reading of how it is kept is mine, from the rules.

E5 · The balance: what ran and what did not

E5 compares four guarantees with every consequence on against every consequence off. Its fifth kernel (governments and labs prevent a silent takeover, everything on) went over the 26 GB cap after the first four had finished, and the claim reads all of them together, so nothing was recorded. By the run rule it is run once more after the memory upgrade. The harness will first be changed to save each kernel's reading as it finishes; that change will be logged.
