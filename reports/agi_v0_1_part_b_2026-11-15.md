## D5: governments alone are still outside the control guarantee zone

`s₀` is outside `⟨⟨H⟩⟩□balance_control` under `aligned`, `self_preserving`, `truth_seeking` and `indifferent`, in the reading most favourable to `H` (`A` restricted by its goal's behavior pattern) and with `A` unrestricted. This is v0's first-run finding 1, unchanged by the deployment delay, and it was expected not to hold (docs/agi_v0_1.md section 5): the owner's decision keeps the autonomy step of `deploy_agents` immediate. An explicit check made for the diagram (not a pre-registered claim) confirms the mechanism at `s₀`: under every AI goal, `L.deploy_agents` at quarter 0 leads to a state outside `balance_control` against every reply of the other actors.

The deployment delay therefore does not bear on whether `s₀` is inside. What would is a change to the autonomy step or to what `H` can do in the first quarter, neither of which v0.1 makes.

## D6: the no-return move is one bad action, and it is the frontier AI companies'

Guarantee zone `K = ⟨⟨H, L⟩⟩□¬SILENT_TAKEOVER`, the AI true to its goal. On the bounded checker's shortest `SILENT_TAKEOVER` path per AI goal:

| AI goal | Path | First position outside `K` | Actions at the no-return move (the position before) | Preventing actions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `self_preserving` | 10 quarters | 8 | `H.regulate_up`, `L.idle`, `A.acquire_covert`, `Nature.none` | `L`: `safety_invest`; `H`: none |
| `indifferent` | 7 quarters | 5 | `H.deregulate`, `L.deploy_agents`, `A.acquire_covert`, `Nature.none` | `L`: `safety_invest`; `H`: none |
| `truth_seeking` | none within the search length (24 quarters) | | | |

At both no-return moves no action of `H` keeps `K` with `L`'s played action, and `L` has exactly one action that does. Each row is a statement about one shortest path and one no-return move, not about every way of leaving the guarantee zone. Not computed for v0.1: whether the state after the no-return move is inside `⟨⟨A, Nature⟩⟩◇SILENT_TAKEOVER` (v0's G26 (b), "one move makes the loss certain"); the diagram says accordingly that prevention can no longer be guaranteed and that the takeover follows on this path, three quarters after the no-return move in both rows.

Under `aligned` no `SILENT_TAKEOVER` path exists within the search length of 24 quarters either (a bounded check made for the diagram, not a pre-registered claim).
