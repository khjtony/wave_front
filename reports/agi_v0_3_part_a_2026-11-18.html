1 · Where the 10 quarters come from

The shortest path to OPEN_TAKEOVER is the same length whatever the frontier AI system wants, because A has no part in it. It is four waits in a row: 4 quarters to AGI level at the frontier AI companies, 2 quarters for the open-weight AI system to follow, 1 quarter to remove its safeguards, 3 quarters of taking control.

What can interrupt this chain is the subject of the guarantee-zone claims: a treaty between states in force before quarter 6 stops the open system upgrade countdown (F3); without the treaty, restricting service cuts the open system's reach only while society's reliance on AI is low (F4).

3 · What the quick claims do not say

  • Nothing about who can prevent it. A shortest path exists whenever nobody stops it. Whether governments, or governments with frontier AI companies, can guarantee no open-weight AI takeover is F3 and F4; whether they can still keep control and peaceful coexistence is F6 and F7.
  • Nothing about likelihood. The 10 quarters need chance to deliver the AGI breakthrough and the retraining at the first opportunity.
  • One setting to keep in mind for the heavy claims: race pressure is on from the first quarter, because at the start state the frontier AI system and the open-weight AI system are both pre-AGI and so equal (section 3a of the pre-registration). The frontier AI companies must deploy or race by the third quarter of any stretch in which either is possible.
