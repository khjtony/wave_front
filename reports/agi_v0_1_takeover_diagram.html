From today to a silent AI takeover: where is the point of no return?

A computer model plays out every move that governments, AI labs and an AI system could make, quarter by quarter, starting today. Nobody can see what the AI really wants, so the model checks four possibilities. For each one it finds the fastest road to a silent takeover (the AI ends up in total control without anyone having noticed) and the last moment at which that could still be reliably prevented.

RESULT D5

Can governments alone keep the AI under control from today?

No, under any of the four objectives. The labs can deploy agents that work unsupervised in the very first quarter, before any new rule can take effect.

RESULT D6

Who holds the last move before the point of no return?

The labs, on both takeover roads. The last chance is a single action, investing in safety, and only the labs can take it. Governments have no move that works alone at that moment.

HOW TO READ THIS

What the model's rules allow, not a forecast

Each road shown is the fastest one the model finds; slower roads exist. "Still preventable" means governments and labs can rule the takeover out whatever the AI and chance do. Past that point they can no longer guarantee it.
