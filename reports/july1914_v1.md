## 1. What v1 changes in the numbers that every claim shares

One number moves across J2v1, J4v1, J7v1 and J8v1. In v0 the coalition without Russia, X = {AUH, GER, FRA, GBR}, left its kernel of `no_war_GER_RUS` along the realized path at **22**, the state after Vienna decided the note on 19 July (reports/july1914.md section 5). In v1 it leaves at **31** under both `fight` pairs, the state of 29 July, after the declaration of war on Serbia of 28 July (played at 30, visible at 31). {AUH} alone leaves at 31 as well (J8v1).

By the guards, this is decision 7e(a). In v1 every Russian mobilization order that a German step reads (`mob_RUS >= partial`) needs `war_AUH_SER == war`: `RUS.mobilize_partial:occasion` (v0) and now `RUS.mobilize_general:austrian_measures`. Only `AUH.declare_war_SER` sets that variable. So, until the declaration, Vienna can still withhold the occasion for any Russian order and therefore for any German mobilization. J2v1's state perturbation at tick 32 shows the same dependence: X's membership flips on `war_AUH_SER` war → peace as well as on `aims_RUS` fight → deter (v0: only the latter). No run of v1 without 7e(a) was made, so the attribution rests on the guards, not on a computed comparison.
