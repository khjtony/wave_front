title: France adopts a four-year rearmament programme

The French Government today approved a programme of armaments spread over four years at a cost of fourteen milliard francs. The Minister of National Defence said that the programme would renew the equipment of the army, in particular its tanks and artillery.

With the British programme set out in the Statement Relating to Defence in March, both western governments now have funded programmes in hand.
