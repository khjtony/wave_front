In the House of Commons this afternoon the Foreign Secretary said that the reoccupation of the Rhineland zone was a breach of the Treaty of Locarno, but that there was no reason to suppose that the present German action implied a threat of hostilities.

His Majesty's Government would examine the German proposals with care, with a view to a general settlement in Western Europe. Ministers did not propose any military measures, and the House received the statement quietly.
