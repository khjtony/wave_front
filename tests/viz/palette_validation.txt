Palette validation for the visualizer page (wavefront/viz/template/viz.css).
Produced with the data-visualization guidance's validator, scripts/validate_palette.js,
run with node from the guidance's directory. Each block gives the command, then its output.
tests/viz/test_palette.py checks that every color declared in viz.css appears below.

Notes
- Categorical slots: the reference palette's eight hues in its documented order, light and dark.
  Actors take slots in declaration order and are always named in text; trace A and B in the
  compare view take slots 1 and 2. The light-mode sub-3:1 WARN (aqua, yellow, magenta) is
  relieved by the direct labels (actor names on every lane, chip and legend) and the table twins.
- Status: the four reserved status steps are fixed by the guidance and the design (never themed).
  The validator's categorical checks do not apply to them (its scope note says so): run as a
  categorical set, warning (and in dark mode serious) sits outside the lightness band and the
  warning/serious pair falls below the 15 normal-vision floor (13.6) in both modes. As the
  guidance requires, a status color never carries meaning alone: every status cell, badge and
  legend entry pairs it with an icon shape (dot, half ring, quarter ring, cross) and the status
  name in the legend, tooltip and tables.
- Ordinal ramp (variable heatmap, level scale) and sequential ramp (landscape forward/backward):
  the blue ramp, light steps 250..700 and dark steps 600..150 (validated as declared, --ordinal).
  A variable with n levels takes steps round(i*9/(n-1)) of the ten; the n = 2..5 subsets pass
  every ordinal check in both modes. Adjacent 50-unit steps (the full ten, used only when a
  variable has more than five levels, or for continuous depth and rank) sit at delta-L 0.047,
  below the 0.06 gap check: those rows carry selective direct level labels, a scale legend,
  tooltips and table twins.
- Nature uses the de-emphasis gray #898781 (the muted ink token), not a categorical slot.
